---
title: `locate()` has no far end — a car past the start or finish reads as ON ROAD forever
date: 2026-08-27
scope: engine/game/track.ts
concepts: [collision, terrain, physics, off-road]
---

`locate` finds the nearest centerline sample and calls the car off-road on its
LATERAL offset alone. At either end of the stage the nearest sample stays
nearest however far past it the car gets, and the lateral offset of a car
reversing straight back from the grid is zero — so the car was reported on-road
a kilometre behind the start line, held at the start's elevation on a flat
invisible ribbon running to the horizon: floating over valleys, buried in
hillsides, driving through both. It is the same bug class as any nearest-point
projection with no longitudinal bound, and it is invisible to every test that
only drives forward.

The fix is `pastApron` in `track.ts`: at `index === 0` (or the last sample of a
FINITE track — an endless stage's far end is a frontier the stream has not
reached, not an end) project onto the sample's forward axis and call it off-road
past `STAGE_RULES.startZone.apron`. The terrain already agrees on that boundary:
`terrain.ts`'s `nearestSample` measures from the apron's spine for exactly one
apron and then blends out, so the two hand over without a step.

Watch for the knock-on: any scenario that reverses from the grid now leaves the
stage after 30 m and lands on the terrain, which changed `reverse_test`'s
terminal speed. Reverse scenarios have to be staged a few hundred meters up the
road, not at the line.
