---
title: A roll passes through upright once a turn, and charging that arrival a sliding stop ends it there
date: 2026-09-04
scope: engine/game/roll.ts
concepts: [roll, friction, suspension, contacts]
---

`pivotKeep` has always known whether the corner ARRIVING at the ground is
sprung, and handed most of the rotational exchange back for a wheel
(`roll.sprung`, 0.88) — because that is what a suspension is for. The TRAVEL
rub never learned the same thing, and took a full Coulomb drag off every
arrival whatever met the ground.

A rolling body meets its wheels once per revolution. So once a turn, a car
mid-rollover was charged `grip × descent` of sliding friction for a contact
where the tyres are ROLLING: one step took 9.2 m/s out of the 10.2 the car
was carrying into the second turn, and the roll ended there, upright,
"having touched nothing".

The fix is one line and the same argument the rotation already makes —
`const drag = pivot.sprung ? 1 - R.sprung : 1` — but the general lesson is
about where to look. **When a model already makes a distinction on one axis,
check whether the sibling axis makes it too.** The sprung/rigid split was
present, documented, and load-bearing for the rotation; the travel had simply
never been asked.
