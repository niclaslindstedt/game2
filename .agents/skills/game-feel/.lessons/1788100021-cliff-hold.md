---
title: A chase camera that rides a falling car down makes a 25 m cliff read as nothing happening — hold height and let the car sink away
date: 2026-08-28
scope: pwa/src/game/camera.ts
concepts: [camera, cliff, framing, game-feel, jumps]
---

Driving off a cliff, the rig keeps the camera its usual 2 m over the roof for
the whole plunge, so the car does not move in frame and the biggest thing that
can happen on a stage reads as nothing at all. The floor cannot rescue it: the
camera clears the lip about a fifth of a second after the car does, and past
that there is no ground under it either.

The fix is one term — the camera declines to follow part of the drop and lets
the car sink down the frame. Key it to how far the car has fallen BELOW WHERE
IT LEFT THE GROUND (freeze `takeoff = car.y` while grounded, in `update` so
every mode keeps it), never to air time or to `vy`: a lip, a crest and a
designed ramp jump all land near the height they launched from, so a 6 m
deadzone plus a 0.5 share (capped at 12 m) leaves every jump's framing exactly
as it was. `CHASE_RIGS` gets it as a per-rig share — the rigs already 10–20 m
up take a fraction, because from there holding another twelve only makes the
car small. Wind it on fast (5/s — the drop is itself the shape of the gesture)
and off slowly (1.4/s), or the landing is followed by the camera dropping onto
the car like a lift.

No aim change is needed: the rig already aims at the car, so the shot pitches
over the edge by itself.
