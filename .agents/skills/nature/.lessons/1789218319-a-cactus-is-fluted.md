SKILL.md carries the two structural rules this country lives on (a cactus
body is `ribbed`, a wash tree is an open scatter on several stems). Two
smaller ones it does not:

**The arm.** A saguaro's arm leaves the trunk almost horizontally, turns
through an ELBOW over half a metre, and then runs straight up, usually
finishing near the height of the crown it grew from. A right angle reads as
plumbing; a smooth curve reads as the bough of a tree. Build it as a chain of
three short `ribLimb`s at falling tilts, each hinged on the last one's end,
then the vertical rise — the elbow is the entire silhouette and it is what a
player recognises the plant by at four hundred metres.

**The colour.** Cactus skin is a dull, slightly blue grey-green under a waxy
bloom. Authored at a birch's saturation it reads as foliage whatever shape it
is, and at night under a headlight it glows. The distance between cactus
grey-green and tree leaf green is most of what says "desert" at speed, so
every green in the desert half of the paint box is pulled toward grey on
purpose — and the one SATURATED colour in the country is the spring's.

**And a bloom belongs on the part that flowers.** Putting palo verde's April
yellow on the single `PALO_VERDE` colour turned the trunk yellow too, because
the bark and the leaves were the same entry. Bark and crown are separate
colours now (`PALO_VERDE` / `PALO_VERDE_LEAF`) and only the crown is on the
season table.
