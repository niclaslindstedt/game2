Privacy Policy

Scandinavian Flick collects nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no server of ours anywhere that knows you played.

Applies to the web game, the desktop app, and the mobile app. Last updated 2026-09-28.

What the game stores

The game remembers a few things so that it is still the game you left. All of it is held by your own browser or app, on your own device, in ordinary local storage:

  • Your settings — camera, controls, sound, picture quality.
  • Campaign progress and which stages you have opened.
  • Your stage times, the score boards, and the ghost of your own best run.
  • Photographs you take with the in-game camera.

On the web and on the desktop none of it is transmitted: clearing the site data in your browser, or deleting the app, deletes all of it, and there is no copy elsewhere to restore it from.

iCloud, in the mobile app

If you are signed in to iCloud, the iPhone and iPad app also keeps a copy of your stage times and score boards, your split times, your campaign progress and each car's distance driven in your own iCloud storage, so the game on your other devices picks up where you left off. That copy lives in your Apple Account and is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; we have no access to it. Your settings, ghosts, photographs and performance measurements are never copied — they stay on the device that made them. Turning iCloud off for the app in your device's settings stops the copying.

What the game sends

Nothing to us. The mobile and desktop apps ship the whole game inside the download and serve it locally, so they run in airplane mode from the first launch and make no network request in order to play; the mobile app's only traffic is the iCloud copy above, between your device and your own Apple Account. The web version loads the game's own files from its own address once, then runs offline.

The only other connection you can cause is a link you press yourself, which opens in your own browser and is then between you and that site.

Children

The game is suitable for a general audience and collects no personal information from anybody, of any age. There is nothing to buy inside it and no way to communicate with another player through it.

Hosting

The web version is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web server, it sees the requests that fetch the page. We neither receive nor keep those logs, and we run no other service that the game talks to.

Changes

If a future version of the game ever gains something that does leave your device, this page changes in the same release that adds it, and the store listing says so too.
