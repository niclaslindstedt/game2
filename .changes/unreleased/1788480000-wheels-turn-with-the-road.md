type: Changed
title: Wheels turn with the road, and only driven ones spin
---

Only the wheels the engine actually drives can spin up now: a front-driver
lights its front pair up off the line while its rears say the car has not
moved yet, a rear-driver does the opposite, and the four-wheel-drive spreads
the same torque over all four. Every wheel otherwise turns at the speed of
the ground under that corner of the car — the inside pair of a corner turns
slower than the outside pair, and the front wheels visibly slow when
opposite lock drags them sideways across a drift.
