title: A wheel torn off a car is a body of its own — it bounces, rolls on ahead at road speed, keels over and settles
---

A wheel that comes off no longer tumbles like a torn panel. It leaves at the corner's own speed with its tread still turning, bounces on its tyre, lands rolling and runs on down the road for a long way, wanders as it slows, keels over and spins itself down flat. It has a weight, a grip, and the gyroscopic stiffness of a real wheel, and it meets things: the ground it was thrown over, the car it came off (which it can bounce back off), and the trunks and stones beside the road. Every car on the road loses its wheels this way, the field's included. At DETAIL LOW nothing rolls — the corner drops onto its hub and the wheel is simply gone.
