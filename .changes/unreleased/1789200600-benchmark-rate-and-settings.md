title: The benchmark draws the frame rate too, and prints what the picture was set to
---

DEVELOPER ▸ BENCHMARK now draws two lines instead of one. The gold line is
the score as it settles, the same as before. The blue line through it is the
frame rate of each reading on its own — no averaging, no memory — and it is
the one that says WHEN: a machine that spends the second half of the race at
half the frame rate shows up on the score line only as a long sag with no
place in it. The graph has a second axis on the right reading the same
ceiling in frames a second, and the finished score carries its own average
frame rate under it.

Under the graph, once the run is over, the three PICTURE rows the benchmark
deliberately does not pin: RESOLUTION, DETAIL and DISTANCE. Running it twice
with one row moved is the whole use of the tool, and now the screenshot of a
score says which run it was.
