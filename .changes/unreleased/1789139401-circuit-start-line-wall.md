title: No more invisible wall off a circuit's start line
---

Every lap stage — First Light, Ridge Ring, Morning Loop, Village Loop,
Blacktop Ring, Cold Dawn — had a step in the road either side of its
start line that nothing drew: the ground was held level with the line
while the lap fell away under it, so drifting a metre wide off the line
met a ledge up to a car's height, and took the wheels off a car that had
done nothing wrong. The ground now follows the road it is under.
