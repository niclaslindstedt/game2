type: Changed
title: Exhaust follows the DETAIL setting
---

The smoke out of a tailpipe is now part of OPTIONS ▸ PICTURE ▸ DETAIL, on
its own ladder beside the dust: LOW takes the exhaust off every car on the
road, MEDIUM leaves it to the car you are driving, and HIGH puts a whole
grid's worth of it back on the start line. It takes hold the moment you set
it, and a stop that switches it off stops drawing the cloud at all rather
than merely leaving it empty.
