title: The map leans all the way to the ground, and reframes on a new stage
---

The stage map now zooms in to a metre off the ground — close enough to look
at a single tuft, a chipping or a marker post — where it used to stop five
metres out, just short of where any of that becomes legible.

And stepping the seed on Roam, or opening another level in the map viewer,
frames the whole of the new stage again instead of arriving leaned in on a
corner of the one before it. On Roam's own pane the slow turn picks up from
the first frame; a map being read as a measurement — full screen, or with a
layer painted on it — still holds still.
