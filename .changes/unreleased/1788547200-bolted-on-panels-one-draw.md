type: Changed
title: Cheaper cars to draw
---

Every car's bolt-on panels — the bumpers, the bonnet, the hatch, the mirrors and the wing — are now drawn as one piece for as long as they are all still bolted on, and only split back into their own pieces once an impact tears one off. Nothing looks different; a heads-up grid of fifteen cars just costs the phone forty-eight fewer draw calls a frame.
