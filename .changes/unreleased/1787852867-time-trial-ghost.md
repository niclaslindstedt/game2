title: Time trial ghosts
---

Your best run on a stage now comes back out as a ghost: a see-through copy
of the car you set the time in, driving the stage again exactly the way you
drove it, with a chip beside the clock counting the metres of road between
you. It is a picture, not a rival — the ghost runs its own game, so the two
cars can never touch. A best set in the campaign is the one a time trial
puts on the road.
