type: Fixed
title: The ridge between a car's tracks in snow is pressed, not standing
---

The strip a car straddles between its two wheel ruts was drawn standing level
with the untouched field beside it, as though nothing had been over it. The
car's body has: it parts the snow along the line its nose sweeps before the
wheels reach it, so the ridge between the ruts is pressed too, just less than
the ruts themselves. It now stands where it actually stands — a crown, but
well below the walls of snow thrown up either side, rather than a ridge the
car appeared to have driven around.
