type: Changed
title: The temperature is a slider now
---

Roam's TEMPERATURE row runs from -40 °C to +40 °C a degree at a time instead of stopping at five-degree rungs, so the couple of degrees either side of freezing — where the road glazes and the lakes go over — can actually be set. It follows the season's own air in the country until it is moved, and moving the SEASON hands it back. It also now reaches the stage you drive, which it did not before.
