title: The results card carries the whole field, paged, with a picture of every car
---

The card a stage ends on now puts RETIRE, SPECTATE and the way on together
at its head and the stage's whole classification on the card itself — every
crew with a picture of their car, their time and their points, eight to a
page (fewer on a phone held sideways), opened on the page your own row is on
and filling in from the moment the line is crossed — in place of the FULL
RESULTS button.
