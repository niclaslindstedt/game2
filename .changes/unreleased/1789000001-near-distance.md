title: NEAR draw distance is now actually near
---

The shortest stop of OPTIONS ▸ PICTURE ▸ DISTANCE was two-thirds of the
design point, which is not a picture anybody could tell apart from the tuned
one — a player who reached for it because the game was stuttering got a
frame that looked the same and cost very nearly the same. It is now four
tenths: on a clear day the fog closes around two hundred metres out instead
of five hundred, and every road chunk and wild cell past that is dropped
before the frame is drawn rather than shaded into solid fog.

The setting and the weather shorten the same fog and they compound, so there
is now a floor under what the SETTING may take — about five seconds of road
at rally pace. A night downpour on NEAR used to be a wall; it is held at the
weather's own distance instead. The floor never pulls the fog in, only
stops the row pushing it further, so a thunderstorm is still as short as a
thunderstorm is.
