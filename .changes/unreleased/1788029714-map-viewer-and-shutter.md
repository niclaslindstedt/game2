title: A map viewer, a shutter for it, and a car on the map
---

The car is now drawn on the map. It never was, on the grounds that at a whole
stage's framing it is a speck — but its headlights were thrown regardless, so
after dark the map showed a pool of light travelling along an empty road.

The map's slow turn also stays put now until you have zoomed back out to the
whole stage: leaned in, that gentle turn walks the ground straight past the
thing you leaned in to look at.

For developers, the map grows two things. **MAP VIEWER**, on the developer
menu, lists the campaign's own stages by country and opens any of them on the
full-screen map — the roads a player actually drives, rather than whatever
Roam's dials were last left on. And **SCREENSHOT**, on the map's own strip,
saves the whole screen with the debug boxes, the layer legend and the REPRO
line painted into the picture, so a report of a problem in a stage carries
everything needed to reproduce it without a word typed under it.
