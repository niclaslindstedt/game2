title: Split boards flash under the mirror, and remember their record
---

A split board now reports under the rear-view mirror rather than in the
clock's corner, and reports the SEGMENT — how long the piece of road you have
just driven took — with the board's number beside it and the gap to whoever
you are chasing under it. Nothing is called a "CP" any more.

Take that piece of road quicker than it has ever been taken on this machine
and the flash says NEW RECORD! in green. The book is kept per stage and per
board, banked the moment the board goes by rather than at the finish, and the
time it holds is never shown: the news is that you beat it, not by how much.

The co-driver's corner calls have lost their words. The sign is the corner
itself — its shape, its colour, and the point on the side the road turns
toward — which is the half of the call a driver at rally pace was reading
anyway.
