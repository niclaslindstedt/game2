title: A roll lane on the training ground
---

The training ground has a new exercise, R1: a marked lane with a low
concrete rail down each edge, thirty metres of clear run-out with distance
boards across it, and a debris field at the end. Arrive at a rail properly
sideways and it catches the car under its centre of mass and puts you over —
which is how a rally car actually rolls — and then you get to see how far the
roll carries and what happens when it runs into something.
