title: The car flies with its own momentum, rolls when landed sideways, and spins when over-driven
---

The body now carries its own vertical momentum over the ground: a brow taken
at pace goes light, hops, or throws the car, a steepening descent lets it
fly, and driving off a cliff or down a hill carries it out with the speed it
had instead of gluing it to the face. A jump landed crossed up trips the car
over its outside wheels — the tyres bite, the top keeps going, and it rolls.
And a drift held past what the wheel asked for keeps coming on its own
unless it is caught: the lever held at rally pace is a spin, and a spin
goes round — through backwards and on until the speed is scrubbed out of
it, so a car that is lost ends up facing the way it came, at walking pace.
Keeping the car on the road is something the driver does. The bots read the
road for a brow, brake before a jump for the corner past it, and reach for
the lever only when the slide can still scrub the speed.
