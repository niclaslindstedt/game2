title: The front-driver rebuilt
---

The front-wheel-drive hatch is a new car. Its proportions are measured off
period side and rear elevations rather than judged by eye — a much shorter
bonnet, a nose that falls away instead of running level into a blunt wall,
near-equal overhangs, and big wheels inside arches that only just clear
them.

The BACK of it carries the detail, since that is the panel the chase camera
holds for a whole stage: a real tailgate with a shut line round it, a swage
and a grab recess across it, three-cell lamp clusters low at the corners,
a valance with reversing lamps under the bumper, and a pipe out of the left.

The wheels are rims now rather than discs painted on the tyre — a flange
standing proud, a sunk barrel, five spokes with sides you can see, studs —
and the tyres wear a real block tread instead of eight lugs. The cage behind
the glass braces both ways.

It races in white over blue with a yellow hairline and a door roundel.
