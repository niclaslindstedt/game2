title: Privacy policy and support pages
---

The site now carries a privacy policy at `/privacy/` and a support page at
`/support/`. The policy says the one thing that is true of every build: nothing
is collected and nothing leaves your device — your settings, campaign progress,
score boards, ghosts and saved photographs are all held by your own browser or
app. The support page says where to write, and answers the two questions that
account for most of the mail (the game does not need the internet, and your
times live on your own device).
