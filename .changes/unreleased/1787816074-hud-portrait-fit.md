title: Phone HUD fits the screen
---

In portrait the booster now stands upright beside the dials instead of
being pushed off the right edge, the build stamp hangs under the top bar
rather than off the bottom of the screen, and the off-road TRACK button
moved down beside the OFF ROAD flag so the top bar holds a five-digit
stage number and the clock at every phone width. On iOS the game paints the
full screen — the dead band along the bottom of an installed PWA is gone,
while a browser tab keeps the visible viewport it can actually reach.
