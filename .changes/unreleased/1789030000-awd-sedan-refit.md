title: The four-wheel-drive refitted to its elevation — shorter overhangs, a lower nose, wraparound lamps
---

A second and third fitting of the all-wheel-drive sedan against its side elevation, measured again on a finer grid: the nose cap and both bumpers pulled in to where the photograph has them, the bonnet and the cowl brought down to their measured heights, the headlamps dropped a hand to a slim band that wraps round the corners onto the fenders with an amber trailing end, the air dam in body colour, and the lamp pods sized to the nose. The bonnet's lip stops a hand above the real one, because the engine plants roadside stones as solids against the lowest bonnet in the catalog and that bar is a world constant.
