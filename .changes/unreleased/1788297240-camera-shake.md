type: Changed
title: Hitting something shakes the car, not the camera
---

Running into a tree, a boulder or a kerb block no longer rattles the picture
from the outside cameras. The car takes the hit — it crushes, dips its nose
and rocks on its springs — and the shot holds still and lets you watch it.
From behind the wheel, on the bonnet or on the bumper nothing changes: the
driver's head is still thrown at whatever corner of the car took the blow,
which in there is the whole of what says you hit something.

What the outside cameras do still answer — a landing, a ford, a respawn — is
a good deal quieter than it was, and it is now a short decaying wobble rather
than a fresh random offset every frame, so it reads as the car being dropped
rather than as the picture breaking.
