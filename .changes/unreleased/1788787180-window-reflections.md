DETAIL ▸ HIGH now strikes a real reflection on every car's windows: the sky
they are actually pointed at, the horizon lying across the glass at the angle
the pane is raked, cloud sliding through it, and the sun smearing down the
greenhouse as the car turns. The dirt on the glass catches the same sky as
scattered glare, so a screen the stage has caked still reads as glass — and
the arc a wiper cuts through it reflects sharply again. Every level below
HIGH keeps the gradient baked into the panes.
