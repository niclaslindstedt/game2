title: The rattle in the engine and the gravel
---

The car no longer buzzes and rattles as it revs. The engine and the tyre
bed are made of short overlapping grains, and where those grains met at the
wrong moment they fought each other instead of adding up: the engine's level
swung by up to 7 dB across the rev range and shimmered nine times a second,
and the gravel's crunch shook like a maraca at speed. Both now hold steady.

Gravel is also quieter and deeper — the surface a whole rally is driven on
sits under the engine now instead of over it.
