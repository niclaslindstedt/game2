type: Changed
title: The back wiper keeps up with the road
---

The backlight's arm now goes at the first film rather than waiting for the
window to close over, and it throws itself across about twice as fast. On a
gravel stage that is a wiper working — a stroke every second or so, the fan
kept as glass instead of clearing a caked screen once a lap — which is what
the mirror is looking at all stage. The corners no blade reaches still cake,
so the shape on the back window is the same one: a clean half disc cut out
of a filthy pane.

The windscreen is unchanged. It is the pane being looked THROUGH, and an arm
swinging across the view every time a haze settles is worse than the haze.
