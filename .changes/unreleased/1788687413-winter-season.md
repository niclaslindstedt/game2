title: Winter, and the cold
---

A fourth season and a TEMPERATURE dial on Roam: below freezing the taiga and the alps are snow on every loose road and deep snow beside them that the car sinks into, ploughs and leaves tracks in, the rain falls as flakes that settle on the windscreen until the cabin melts them, snow roads slide between gravel and the old alpine ice, and the desert gets its wet season with puddles and greener scrub.
