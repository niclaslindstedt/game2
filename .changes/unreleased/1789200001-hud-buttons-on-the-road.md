type: Changed
title: The buttons on the top bar
---

The way back to the last checkpoint and the camera are twice the size — half
the height of the minimap beside them — and the checkpoint button lights up
red the moment the car is off the road. The camera and the shutter are gone
from the top bar on anything with a keyboard: both have keys, and the camera
is a row on the options page. On a phone, take pictures the way you take
them anywhere else.
