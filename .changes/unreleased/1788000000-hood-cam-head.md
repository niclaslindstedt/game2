title: The hood cam shows the hood, from a head that rides a neck
---

HOOD is now the view from the driver's seat over the car's own bonnet —
the body used to be hidden entirely, so the one camera named after a part
of the car was the one that never showed it. The mount is read off each
car's own silhouette, so a low sedan seats the shot lower than an upright
hatch does and every car shows its own nose.

What you look through is a HEAD rather than a bolt: a mass on a neck that
lags the car and settles a beat after it, thrown forward and nodded down
under the brakes, leaning through a corner, plunging and rebounding through
a landing, with the road's grain buzzing up through it — hard on gravel,
smooth on asphalt, punishing across open country, and silent the moment the
wheels leave the ground. In a slide the driver glances where the car is
actually going instead of staring down the nose.
