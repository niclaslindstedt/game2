title: Rain on the door windows
---

From the cockpit the side glass is now wet too. A door window is edge-on
to the air, so it behaves nothing like the screen: standing on the grid it
only beads, and once the car is rolling every drop on it is blown BACK
along the car in long horizontal streaks — faster and longer than the
screen's runs, because there is no rake to climb. There is no wiper over a
door, so they stream for the whole stage.
