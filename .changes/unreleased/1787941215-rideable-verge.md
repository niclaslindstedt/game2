title: The ground beside the road no longer stops the car
---

A rally car spends half a stage off the road, and the ground next to it was
full of things it could not get back over: hillsides cut off at the verge,
mountain toes blended into a cliff at the road's edge, corner mounds a car
could only stop against, and — where a branch road had wandered back over
the stage — a wall of tarmac hanging forty metres in the air. The landscape
within reach of a road is now held under a cone the car can always drive up,
and the ground tiles can no longer drag a hillside up through the tarmac.
