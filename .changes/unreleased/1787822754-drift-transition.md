---
type: Changed
title: Turning into a drift is now one continuous motion
---

The car no longer has two states. Where a tenth of a turn of the wheel used to take it from a 2° lean to a 32° drift, the angle now moves with the lock the whole way up the throw — at 119 km/h, quarter lock is 4°, half is 8°, three-quarters is 21° and full lock is 35° — with the hand-over from grip to slide eased in so there is no moment where the car changes what it is doing. Unwinding out of a deep drift lets the rotation outlive the lock a little, so the nose swings just past centre and a big slide can ask for a dab of opposite lock on the way out.
