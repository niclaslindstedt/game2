title: A rollover carries the momentum it arrived with
---

A car going over used to be handed energy it never had — a body between
contacts was re-seated onto whatever ground it happened to be flying over, so
it climbed hills for free, and one hand-over from one pivot corner to the next
was charged as four separate impacts on the steps around it, none of which the
body actually made. A crash off a lip could gain a fifth of its whole budget
that way, and the friction had quietly been tuned up to stop it again.

Both are fixed, and the shell's grip re-read against what accident
reconstruction actually measures. Rolls now carry the speed they arrived with:
a trip that used to lurch onto one flank and stop goes over twice and travels
fifty metres doing it, slow trips commit instead of rocking back, and the
whole-crash retardation lands where a real rollover is measured rather than
half again over it.
