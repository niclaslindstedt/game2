---
type: Changed
title: Red-and-white markings go where they mean something
---

Rally kerbing used to run down both edges of every gravel road from the start
line to the finish. Now it is placed the way a real stage marks one: at the
apex of a corner worth aiming at, on the outside where that corner unwinds,
as a braking marker in front of a hard one, and wrapped around a bridge or a
jump lip. Gravel gets marker posts and anti-cut blocks rather than a painted
band — the continuous kerb is for the tarmac sections.
