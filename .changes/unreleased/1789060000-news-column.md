type: Changed
title: The car's news moves out of the way
---

Damage calls, split misses and clean-air calls no longer go up across the
middle of the screen. They stack in the bottom-right corner instead — small,
newest at the bottom, popping up under the line before it. Five stand at a
time and each one holds for fifteen seconds, so news that arrived while you
were busy with a corner is still there when you look up.
