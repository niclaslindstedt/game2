title: No more text loupe on a double tap
---

iOS answered a double tap on the game with its caret-positioning magnifier,
and a held thumb with the COPY / LOOK UP sheet. Both are off everywhere now,
on every screen rather than just the splash card — the three surfaces that
actually take typed text (the Roam seed, the initials entry and the debug
log) keep their caret and their selection.
