title: Restarting hands over a fresh car
---

Restarting a stage now puts a whole car on the road: the dents, the missing
panels, the flat tyres and the last attempt's dirt went with the old attempt
instead of being carried into the new one. The way home does the same when it
lands on the start line — before the first split board there is no road to
lose, so it is the run beginning again and the car begins again with it.
