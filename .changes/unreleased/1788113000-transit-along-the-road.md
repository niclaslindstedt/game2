title: The camera slides back up the road instead of arcing over it
---

The move onto a rival's car after your own run ends now follows the ROAD back
to them, low and level, speeding up and slowing down once. It used to strike
out across country on the straight line between the two cars, which meant
climbing seventy metres to clear whatever was in the way and pitching hard
down to keep the ground in frame — a shot nobody could place themselves in.
Following the road costs no altitude at all: the road already goes round the
hills, and it keeps the stage under the camera the whole way.
