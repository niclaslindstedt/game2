title: Gravel thrown sideways off a drift
---

A sliding car now throws a rooster tail of stones out of the side it is
sliding towards, off the wheels on that side — thicker the deeper the slide
and the faster the tyres are being dragged across the road, and harder off
the axle the drivetrain is spinning, so a rear-driver sprays off its tail
and a front-driver off its nose. Stones, not dust: they leave low, arc, and
land, and they are made of whatever ground the car is on.
