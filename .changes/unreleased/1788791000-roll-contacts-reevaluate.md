title: The ground now decides which way a rolling car keeps going over
---

A roll's direction was settled by the trip that started it and by nothing
afterwards. Every contact could only ever damp the rotation — it scaled it
by a number between nought and one — and it spent its friction entirely on
the car's travel, so the blow that put the car down had no say in which way
it came back up. A fast roll is mostly flight with contacts in it, so that
was the ground's only say, and it never got it: a car that had swapped ends
mid-accident went on turning the way it left the lip while its travel said
the opposite, which is not something a falling car can do.

An arrival now reads the corner that actually meets the ground — a body out
of a flight has been turning about its own centre with nothing under it, so
that corner is sweeping across the car on top of whatever the car itself is
doing — and turns the body the way the ground is dragging it. Rollovers
that used to run to a hard stop in one direction now check, hunt and change
hand the way they do when you watch a real one.
