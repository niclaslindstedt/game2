title: The benchmark runs a stage where every video row shows
---

The benchmark ran a stage the DISTANCE row could barely move, and a row that
cannot move the number is a row the tool cannot report on: a player walks it
end to end, reads the same score three times, and concludes it does nothing.

Metered at the fog's two ends, draw calls a frame:

| stage       | NEAR | FAR |          |
| ----------- | ---- | --- | -------- |
| Mill Bridge | 503  | 665 | +32%     |
| Bajada      | 484  | 486 | 0%       |
| Arroyo      | 495  | 719 | **+45%** |

An open stage is not the answer — Bajada's flats see a long way and find
almost nothing out there. What is needed is depth with things in it, and
Arroyo is that: five kilometres of desert with the saguaro standing in it,
where the row moves nearly half the frame's draw calls and two thirds again
of its triangles.

**Scores from earlier builds do not compare to these.** An index is a time
for a fixed piece of work, and the work has changed. The card names the
stage it ran, which is what makes two screenshots tell you whether they can
be compared at all.
