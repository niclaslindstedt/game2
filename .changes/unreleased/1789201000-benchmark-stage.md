title: The benchmark runs a stage where every video row shows
---

The benchmark ran the campaign's first stage, and on a tree-lined sprint the
DISTANCE row is worth about 13% of what the frame submits — which vanishes
under the ~10% two runs of the same build differ by. So a player could walk
that row end to end, read the same score three times, and conclude it did
nothing.

Metered at the fog's two ends, draw calls a frame:

| stage          | NEAR | FAR |          |
| -------------- | ---- | --- | -------- |
| Loggers' Run   | 203  | 229 | +13%     |
| Bajada         | 174  | 183 | +5%      |
| Creosote Flats | 222  | 365 | **+64%** |

An open stage is not the answer either — Bajada's flats see a long way and
find almost nothing out there. What is needed is depth with things in it,
and Creosote Flats is that: the row moves two thirds of the frame's draw
calls and half its triangles.

**Scores from earlier builds do not compare to these.** An index is a time
for a fixed piece of work, and the work has changed. The card names the
stage it ran, which is what makes two screenshots tell you whether they can
be compared at all.
