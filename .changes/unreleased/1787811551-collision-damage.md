type: Added
title: Collision and damage
---

Cars now bend and break instead of teleporting: hitting trees, boulders and
fallen trunks bounces and spins the car and permanently crumples its body —
mirrors, bumpers and the wing tear off and tumble down the road. Trees are
solid now, on the treeline and deep in the wild alike. Hard landings crush
the underside too. Damage reaches the machinery — engine, suspension,
gearbox and steering each degrade their own feel — and a spent chassis
wrecks (respawn, patched half-way back). A new damage instrument in the HUD
cluster shows the 2D car with its dents, the torn-off parts, and one meter
per internal system.
