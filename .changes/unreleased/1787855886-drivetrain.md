title: The three cars now drive like three different cars
---

Front-, rear- and four-wheel drive are real physics now — the rear-driver
will hang its tail out at 10 km/h, the front-driver straightens itself on
the throttle and has to be flicked or lifted into a slide, and the
four-wheel-drive puts its power down on anything — and each car is fastest
on a different kind of stage.
