title: Contrails and cirrus
---

The airliners over a stage now leave the trail a photograph of one shows: two hair-thin lines at the wings that merge and spread into a broad faint band, straight to the end, from a speck of an aeroplane crossing slowly enough to read as ten kilometres up. The cirrus over the forest, the desert and the Alps is combed into fibres, the noon sky is a deeper blue, and the sky and traffic contact sheets no longer photograph a car's headlamps that is not there.
