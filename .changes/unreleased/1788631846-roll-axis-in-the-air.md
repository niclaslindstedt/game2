title: A car going over turns about its own weight in the air, not about the ground under it
---

A rolling car between its contacts now turns about its centre of mass: the weight flies straight and the wheel plane swings round it, out to the side as the car comes onto its flank and back under it on the roof. Before, the wheel plane flew straight and the body's weight was slung round it on the arm of its own height, which read as a car rotating on an axis somewhere below it.
