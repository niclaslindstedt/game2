type: Fixed
title: No magnifier over the road
---

On iPhone and iPad the game no longer answers a press-and-hold with the text
magnifier and its caret. It was appearing under a thumb on the throttle or
the wheel — taking the touch with it, so the gas cut out and the steering
stopped answering mid-corner. Buttons, menus and the fields you type your
initials into are untouched.
