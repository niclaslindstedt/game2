---
type: Changed
title: The developer map's own controls
---

The full-screen map now fills the pane rather than covering the screen, so
its layer switch, its shutter and the gesture hint all sit inside the frame
instead of off the edge of a phone. The hint moved from the top corner —
where a phone's status bar ate it — to just above the layer buttons, and
COPY DEBUG INFO became a mark in the header, in the slot that used to spell
out ROAM.
