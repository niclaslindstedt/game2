type: Added
title: An engine under every bonnet
---

Lose a bonnet and there is now an engine bay behind it instead of a painted
panel: a well cut down into the front of the body, with a block and alloy
rocker cover, a round air cleaner, a radiator across the nose, the battery
in one corner and the fluid bottles in the other, strut towers and a brace
across them. Every car in the catalog gets one, and it crumples with the
nose in a front-end impact. The stripes painted on a bonnet now tear off
with it rather than hanging over the hole.
