title: A GRADIENT dial, and a taiga that runs spring to autumn
---

Roam gets a GRADIENT dial: CLIMB, MIXED or DESCENT. It decides where a
stage starts in its country — high ground for a run that comes down, low
for one that climbs — so a descent is speed the road gives back rather than
speed you have to earn. It cannot tilt a circuit, because a lap comes back
to its own start line.

The taiga's campaign was rebuilt on it. It runs spring to autumn now and
never in winter — the cold country is the alpine, and winter is Roam's — and
its first three levels are wider and fall gently, so there is somewhere to
learn to steer before the road starts asking questions.
