type: Changed
title: The benchmark scores an index, and draws it
---

DEVELOPER ▸ BENCHMARK now reports an INDEX instead of a stopwatch reading:
100 is drawing the race in the time it takes to drive, 200 is twice that,
400 four times. Higher is better, and one machine's score divides into
another's to say how many times faster it is.

The card is a live graph rather than a counter. The score of the run so far
is read every fifteen frames and drawn across the whole run, so the line
settles onto the final number as it goes — and a machine that stumbles
halfway leaves a kink in it that one figure at the end would have hidden.
The score rides the line's leading edge; the finished one stands above the
graph.
