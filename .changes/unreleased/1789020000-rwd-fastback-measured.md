title: The rear-driver rebuilt as the big late-80s fastback
---

The rear-wheel-drive car is a new car, measured off ruled side, front and rear elevations against its wheel centres: a long, low three-door fastback on the longest wheelbase in the catalog, with an aero nose falling to a slot of a grille between rectangular quad lamps, two louvred vents in the bonnet, a flush gutterless glasshouse with a plumb B-pillar and a sail panel beside a huge raked backlight, deep body-coloured bumpers with a black rubbing strip let into them and the same strip down the flank, woven cross-spoke lattice wheels on low-profile tyres, and — the part the chase camera looks at all stage — a whale tail on posts at its ends over the tailgate, the evolution car's second lip under it, tail clusters running amber, red, white, red across their width, a plate between them and a pipe out of the left.
