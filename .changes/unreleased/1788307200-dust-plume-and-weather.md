title: The dust plume, mud and tyre soot
---

A gravel stage now hangs a boiling cloud of dust behind the car from 30 km/h up, thickening the faster you go and towed along in the car's own wake — backwards when you reverse. Rain takes it away entirely and puts dark clods of mud off the wheels in its place, with the tyre bed turning from rattling gravel into a heavy wet churn and the rain itself audible over the whole run. On tarmac there is still no cloud at all, only tyre smoke — and a slide held long enough now cooks the rubber and turns that smoke black.
