title: Screenshots, and a gallery to keep them in
---

Press ENTER mid-stage and the game takes a picture of the frame you are
looking at — or tap the shutter on the HUD's button row, which is how it is
reached on a phone. Every picture is signed with the Scandinavian Flick mark
in the corner, so a drift that ends up in a chat window still says what game
it came from.

The pictures land in MAIN MENU ▸ GALLERY: one shot fills the page, the roll
runs as a filmstrip under it, and the arrow keys flip between them. From
there each one can go to your phone's share sheet, onto the clipboard, or
into your downloads — whichever of the three the browser you are on can
actually do. The roll keeps the last forty and the oldest falls off.

Not for you? OPTIONS ▸ CONTROLS ▸ SCREENSHOTS switches the key and the
shutter off. It ships on, and the gallery stays reachable either way — the
pictures you have already taken are still yours.
