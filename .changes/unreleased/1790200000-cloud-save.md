title: Your boards follow you between your own devices
---

In the **App Store app**, your stage boards, sector records, campaign ladder
and odometers are carried between your own Apple devices through iCloud —
nothing to sign into, no account to make, and no server of ours in the middle.

Drive on one device while the other is in a pocket and both keep their work:
the faster time wins each board, the further rung wins the ladder, and a car's
distance only ever goes up. Ghosts and benchmarks stay where they were set —
one is too big for the store and the other measures the machine you set it on.
