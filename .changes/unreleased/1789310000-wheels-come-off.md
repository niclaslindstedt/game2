title: A car sheds its wheels where it landed on them
---

Which corner an arrival goes down through is now the attitude the car came
down at, rather than an even quarter each: a car that spears into a valley
nose-first tears its front wheels off and leaves the rear pair flat and
hanging, and one slammed onto its left flank loses the left pair. And a
wheel torn off is no longer dropped — it is squeezed out from between the
ground and its own arch, so how hard it leaves is how hard the thing that
took it off was travelling, and the corner the car landed on throws its
wheel furthest. A wheel levered off a hub at road speed still leaves
exactly as gently as it always did.
