A rally rating for generated stages, and an audit for the campaign ladder.
`make rate` scores a seed on what makes a rally stage good rather than merely
correct — its flow, its pace and how much the speed moves, the vertical and
what it does to the corners, its set pieces, the country around it and what a
mistake costs — and `make rate CAMPAIGN=1` judges a location's six levels
together, on whether the ladder climbs, whether its stages are unlike each
other, and whether the weather, the calendar and all three cars get used.
