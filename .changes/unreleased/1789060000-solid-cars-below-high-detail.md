type: Changed
title: Rival cars are built solid below HIGH detail, and every car is on LOW
---

The DETAIL row now decides whose windows can be seen through as well as what is behind them. On MEDIUM the car you are driving keeps its cabin, its glass and its wipers, and every other car on the road is the solid one — opaque windows with nothing built behind them, no wiper arms, no film on the screens — which takes a cabin's worth of triangles and three draw calls off each rival. On LOW that is every car, the player's included: it no longer keeps a cabin and see-through glass for the sake of the cockpit view. HIGH furnishes the whole entry list, as before.
