title: The crowd drove here — car parks off the public roads, and trails to the stands
---

Every stand of spectators is now reached from a car park: a graded pad of
gravel with the bays marked across it and twenty or more cars nosed into
them, off a road that is not the rally road — an abandoned arm past its
barrier, the lane out from another car park, or, where no public road is
within reach, a gravel lane of its own that runs out to the edge of the map.
From the pad a trodden trail leads through the grass to the back of each
stand it serves, with arrow boards along it pointing the crowd the way, and
a P at the gate. The cars are as solid as they look. A stand the country
leaves no room to serve stays unserved, and the stage analyzer's `roads`
metric now reports which ones.
