type: Added
title: Stage minimap and in-race menu
---

The top-right corner now carries a minimap: the whole stage drawn as a
route, the car riding it as an arrowhead, and how far into the run you are
read off the frame itself — the border fills clockwise from the top as you
get further in (an endless run reads the distance covered instead). Tap or
click it for the in-race menu, which holds RESUME, RESTART STAGE and RACE
SETUP; Esc opens the same card on a keyboard. The top bar keeps the stage,
the clock and a camera glyph, and nothing else.
