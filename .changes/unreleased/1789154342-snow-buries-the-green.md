title: Snow buries the green
---

Green grass, flowers and brush no longer stand out of the snow. Anything
soft — the ground cover, the fringe along the road's own shoulder, the brush
between the trunks, the undergrowth round one and a paddock's grass — now
asks whether the ground it would grow on is under snow, measured from the
lower of the country's permanent snowline and the height that stage's air
freezes at. So an alp above its summer snowfields is bare, and the same
valley that grows hay in July grows nothing in a freezing spring. The
forest is untouched: a spruce wood stands through its winter wearing the
load of snow it always did.
