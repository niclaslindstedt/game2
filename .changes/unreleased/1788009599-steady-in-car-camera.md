title: A head on a neck behind the wheel
---

The cockpit, bonnet and nose cameras shook badly enough to be hard to drive
from — worst of all in the cockpit, where the dashboard swam about while the
road ahead barely moved, and worse again on monitors faster than 120 Hz. The
driver's head now sits on an actual neck: hinged below the eyes, stiff and
well damped, and moved by what the driver is being pushed by rather than by
the shell's every rut. It goes back a touch under power and forward under
the brakes, leans out of a corner, and is thrown forward or to the side by a
collision — but never further than a neck reaches, whatever the car hits.
