title: The whole HUD in a screenshot
---

Screenshots now carry everything that was on screen. The news column, the
split board, the touch wheel and every other reading that fades or pops in
were being left out — a picture rendered them from their first frame, which
for all of those is invisible — and the pause card was missing entirely.
The signature in the corner steps clear of them the way it already did the
speedo.
