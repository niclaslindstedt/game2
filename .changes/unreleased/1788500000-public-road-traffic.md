title: Traffic on the public roads
---

The public roads the rally does not close now carry traffic: leave the stage
down an abandoned arm or a car park lane and you meet motorists driving the
posted limit — twenty different vehicles, from a city car to a timber lorry
and an articulated truck — coming in from the edge of the map to park up for
the rally, leaving town for somewhere else, and giving way to whatever is in
the lane ahead of them, you included. You can hit them, and they can be
shoved off their lane or stopped for good by it. Speed limit signs stand
beside every public road, posting 70 in the open, 50 through a village and
30 on the lane into a car park.
