type: Changed
title: The forest's rocks and stumps are solid now
---

Rocks, cut stumps and the bedrock outcrops beside the road used to be
scenery the car drove straight through. They are placed by the engine now
and the car hits them, along with the trees and boulders that always were
solid. The bar is the middle of the hood: anything standing higher than
that stops the car, anything lower is loose stone the wheels ride over.
Nothing solid stands on the road's shoulder or its ditch, so running wide
still costs you the same as it did.
