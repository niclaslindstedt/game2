title: A crash throws up gravel and smoke
---

Going over now looks like it costs something. Every time the shell slams
into the ground a burst of gravel and dust comes off the corner that hit,
and a car grinding along on its roof or its flank ploughs up a continuous
cloud behind it — thicker the faster it is going, and gone as it comes to
rest.

A spin throws far more than it used to as well: four tyres dragged sideways
now throw off all four corners instead of the two a tidy drift hangs out,
which is what makes a car going round backwards at 100 km/h read differently
from a drift held a beat too long.

None of it was possible from the wheels: every ground cloud in the game
spawns at an axle, and a car on its roof has its axles in the air.
