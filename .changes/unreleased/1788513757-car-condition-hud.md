title: Car condition schematic
---

A plan of your own car sits under the minimap and colours itself as the car
takes damage: green is well, yellow is damaged, orange is very damaged, red
is broken. The nose, windscreen, cabin, tail, all four wheels and all four
lamps each carry their own colour, and a row of marks under the car names
the machinery you cannot see — engine, radiator, suspension, gearbox,
steering, brakes — the moment one of them starts giving.
