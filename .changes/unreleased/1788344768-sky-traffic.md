---
type: Changed
title: A sky with traffic in it
---

Aircraft cross the sky over a stage far more often — six or so during a
two-minute race instead of one — and the contrails they leave now hang
there for two and a half minutes, spreading and thinning rather than
vanishing behind them. A stage also OPENS on four trails at different ages,
so the grid stands under somebody else's morning instead of under an empty
sky. Nothing of it is drawn under an overcast deck, where a driver could
not see above the weather anyway.
