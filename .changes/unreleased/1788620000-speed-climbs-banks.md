type: Changed
title: Speed carries the car up a steep bank
---

A bank steeper than the wheels alone can climb is no longer a wall if you arrive fast enough — momentum takes the car up it and the grade drains the speed on the way, and a bank taken far faster than it asks for costs the car's floor — while a face steeper than about seventy degrees still stops the car and folds the nose whatever the speed. The last hillsides the generator left standing too steep beside the road are gone with it: an embankment now falls at one grade and lands on the country, a fill on a hillside is built to reach the ground, the ground between two roads carries the taller fill and the deeper cut of the two, and a stage may no longer pass its own start apron a dozen metres above it.
