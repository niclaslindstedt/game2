title: The rear-view mirror pays for itself
---

The mirror is now redrawn sixty times a second — twice as live as before — on a machine that is holding sixty frames, and gives that back on one that is not: the rate halves, then halves again with the reach pulled in, and climbs back the moment the frames are there again. It is the most expensive thing in a driving frame, so it is the first thing to give way, and what a struggling machine loses is the freshness of the glass rather than the pace of the road.
