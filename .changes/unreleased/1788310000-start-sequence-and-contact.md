title: A start you watch, and rivals you can touch
---

Every stage now opens on the start control: the camera circles the line
while the crew ahead of you gets their green and drives away up the road,
then comes down into your own camera as the gantry lights up. The ten
seconds you spend watching them go are the ten seconds of head start they
have — the rally stagger the results are read off, seen rather than
explained. Any pedal skips the camera and goes straight to the lights.

The field is really out there too. Every rival is on the road in their own
car and their own colours, and they are SOLID: catch the crew in front and
you can lean on them out of a corner, or put them into the trees and take
their time with them. They can do the same to you — both cars bend, both
lose parts, and neither one of you gets the seconds back.
