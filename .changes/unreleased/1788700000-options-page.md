type: Changed
title: Options rebuilt as one page of a dozen knobs
---

OPTIONS is one page off the main menu — every setting the same row with its value between two arrows, one QUALITY setting in place of six video levers, the HUD as one switch plus the rear-view mirror, the keyboard's and controller's bindings on pages of their own, RESTORE DEFAULTS at the bottom — and the pause card now carries the camera, HUD, rear view and volume knobs itself instead of opening the whole page over a run.
