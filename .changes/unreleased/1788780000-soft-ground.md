title: The ground is softer and the road is one surface with the country
---

Leaving the road and coming back onto it is a bump on the springs instead of a snap, banks and cut verges are climbed rather than crashed into, the smallest rocks are litter the wheels ride over and the ones that stay solid are shoved rather than hit like walls, a jump met from behind is climbed and flown off instead of clipped through, and the chase camera settles onto the ground's steps instead of cutting to them.
