title: Dirty side windows, and a heads-up grid that runs
---

Every window on a rally car now gets filthy, not just the two the wipers
reach. The flanks pick it up an order of magnitude more slowly than the
screens do and nothing ever takes it back off, so a car arrives at the finish
with side glass that has a stage on it — and stops short of opaque, so the
crew still show through. The backlight remains the pane that ends a rally
caked, with the windscreen behind it and the flanks last.

A full heads-up race is also a lot cheaper to run. The rivals' screens still
go brown and their wipers still clear them, but at a resolution meant to be
read from another car rather than through — 48 triangles a screen instead of
3,456, which is nearly half the triangles off every car on the grid. Their
cabins build a level down, and their bodies are now generated one per frame
during the establishing shot instead of all at once on the green. CAR DETAIL
on the video page (was CAR INTERIORS) turns the grime off along with the
cabins at OFF. Underneath, the query every car asks hundreds of times a
second — where is the road from here — rejects most of the map before it
reads it, so the whole field costs about a third less to drive.
