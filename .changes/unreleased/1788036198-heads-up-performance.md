title: A heads-up grid that runs
---

A full heads-up race is a lot cheaper to run. The rivals' screens still go
brown and their wipers still clear them, but at a resolution meant to be read
from another car rather than through — 48 triangles a screen instead of
3,456, which is nearly half the triangles off every car on the grid. Their
cabins build a level down, and their bodies are now generated one per frame
during the establishing shot instead of all at once on the green. CAR DETAIL
on the video page (was CAR INTERIORS) now turns the grime off along with the
cabins at OFF. Underneath, the query every car asks hundreds of times a
second — where is the road from here — rejects most of the map before it
reads it, so the whole field costs about a third less to drive.
