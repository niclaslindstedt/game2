type: Changed
title: A heads-up grid that runs
---

A full eight-car heads-up race is a lot cheaper to run. The rivals no longer
carry the grime film on their windscreens or the full roll-caged cabin — you
cannot see either through somebody else's glass — which takes nearly half the
triangles off every car on the grid, and their bodies are now generated one
per frame during the establishing shot instead of all at once on the green.
Underneath, the query every car asks hundreds of times a second — where is
the road from here — rejects most of the map before it reads it, so the whole
field costs about a third less to drive.
