title: The sound, rebuilt — no more crackle, a mix that follows the camera, a country you can hear, and a score for every kind of stage
---

The engine, the tyres, the wind and the weather are now continuous voices steered on the audio thread instead of overlapping bursts, which is what stops the crackle on Bluetooth headphones; the mix moves with the camera from the cabin to the helicopter, the stage now has birds, insects, an owl, livestock, a train, split-board beeps, a wastegate, exhaust crackle and wipers in it, and there are seven scores — one for the menu, and one each for the taiga by day, in the rain, at night, the desert, a circuit and an endless stage.
