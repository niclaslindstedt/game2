title: No speed limit off the road
---

Open country used to stop the car dead at about 150 km/h, whichever car it
was. That ceiling is gone: the wild now charges for itself on the way UP —
a driven wheel digs into unconsolidated ground instead of driving, so a
field is half again as long to 100 km/h as the road is — and charges
nothing at all once the car is up and skimming. Past about 125 km/h there
is nothing holding the top end back but the gearbox, so a long enough run
across the landscape ends at the same 190–240 km/h the stage does, and
whatever is at the end of that run is a jump taken at the speed it was
worth. Corners out there are still a different matter.
