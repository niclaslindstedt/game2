title: Modelled trees
---

Every tree beside the road is now a proper model — spruces and firs hung
with drooping boughs, pines with soft tufted crowns, birches with black
marks on their white bark and leafy, weeping crowns, stone pines, flagged
treeline larches, silver snags, ribbed saguaros and shaggy Joshua trees —
in every season's colours, with lighter versions standing further out in
the wild.
