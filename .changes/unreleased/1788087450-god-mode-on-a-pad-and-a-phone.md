title: God mode can be flown on a controller and on a phone
---

Switching god mode on used to leave a handheld or a touchscreen holding a
steering wheel and a throttle that did nothing: the free camera answered
only to a keyboard, and the driving controls on screen belonged to a car the
tool had just parked. The same two thumbs now fly instead — a push stick
under the steering thumb, drag-to-look under the other, and buttons for up,
down and the cruise speed — and on a controller the stick you steer with
moves the camera, the other one aims it, the triggers climb and descend, the
handbrake button is the sprint and the shift shoulders turn the cruise
speed. Nothing has to be bound first: the flight is read off the driving
bindings you already have.
