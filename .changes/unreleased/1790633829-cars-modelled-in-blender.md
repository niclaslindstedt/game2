type: Added
title: The cars are modelled in Blender
---

Every car is now a model made in Blender off its own numbers: rounded panels
with crisp shut lines and belt lines, bumpers that wrap the corners, turned
tyres with a shouldered tread and deeper rims. It still wears its livery,
still gets filthy over a stage, and still folds, scuffs and sheds its bumpers,
doors and bonnet in a crash exactly as before.
