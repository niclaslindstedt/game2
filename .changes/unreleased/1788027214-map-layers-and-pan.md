type: Added
title: X-ray the map, and walk around it
---

Roam's map now pans and zooms without limit: hold ⌘/CTRL and drag — or use
two fingers, or the middle button — to walk the map sideways, and keep
scrolling to lean in until the lens is a few metres off the ground, close
enough to see the road surface and the trees beside it.

With the developer menu out, the map also X-rays the stage. Five layers of
the ground it was built on — bedrock, groundwater, soil, foliage and roads —
paint over the landscape with a legend saying what each band is worth, and
FULL SCREEN blows the pane up to the whole window with a box naming the seed,
the dials, what the stage was assembled from, what the layer measured, and a
link that puts anyone else in front of the same map.
