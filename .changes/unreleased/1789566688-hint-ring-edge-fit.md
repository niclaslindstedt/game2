title: The pedal hint stays on the screen
---

Anchoring a thumb near the edge of the glass used to hang the word naming
the gesture off the screen, so the one thing that teaches the touch controls
was missing exactly where a one-handed grip puts the thumb. The hint ring is
now nudged back into the frame when it would overhang; a thumb with room to
spare still gets its ring drawn precisely where it pressed.
