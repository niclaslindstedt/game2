type: Added
title: Confetti and smoke cannons at the finish
---

Your stage time is now classified against the stage's start list, and a
podium finish is saluted: cannons beside the finish gate throw confetti and
coloured smoke over the car as it comes through, in descending amounts for
first, second and third. Fourth and back get the quiet they earned. The
results card shows the placing and the gap to the crew above you.
