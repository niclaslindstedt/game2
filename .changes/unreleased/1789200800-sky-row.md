title: SKY is its own row in OPTIONS ▸ VIDEO
---

The sky was the tenth lever on the DETAIL row, and it did not belong there:
everything else on that row is paid per OBJECT, and the sky is paid per
PIXEL — every octave of cloud noise over a third of the frame. It is the
dearest thing in the panel after RESOLUTION, and it was the one lever nobody
could reach on its own.

Now it is its own row: SIMPLE is the arcade sky, LAYERED is the cloud chart
at its real altitudes with the valley mist and the mountain's shadow, and
FULL lights every cloud edge by a second sample toward the sun and throws
their shadows across the ground. It ships LAYERED, the design point.

Which means a machine that is short of frames can now find out WHY: thin the
forest if it is bound by how much it is drawing, flatten the sky if it is
bound by how many pixels it is shading. Those are different machines, and
under one preset neither could say so.

A sky already chosen on the old ladder is kept.
