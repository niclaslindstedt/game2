type: Changed
title: The camera watches a roll from the verge
---

When the car goes over, the outside cameras now stop travelling with it,
come to rest at the side of the road and watch the accident as a bystander —
zooming to keep the car a readable size as it tumbles away, climbing to see
over anything it goes behind, and flying back to the driving view once it has
stopped; the three cameras inside the car go over with it as before.
