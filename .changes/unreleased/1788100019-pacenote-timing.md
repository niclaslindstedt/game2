type: Changed
title: Steadier corner calls
---

The co-driver's sign now goes up about two seconds before the corner instead
of a few hundred metres out, stays up until the car is through it, and comes
back down — no more signs appearing, vanishing under braking and reappearing
on the way out. The corner after this one is only shown when it lands in the
same couple of seconds, drawn half transparent underneath; anything further
up the road waits its turn.
