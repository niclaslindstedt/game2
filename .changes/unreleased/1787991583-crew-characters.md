title: Sixteen crews behind the glass
---

The people in the cars are people now. Sixteen characters — yours, the
campaign's fourteen rivals, and a privateer for anybody else — built to
match who they are: Granite fills the cabin, Skarv is all neck, Diesel does
not fit behind his own wheel, Old Snow drives in a flat cap and a white
beard, and Sideways has no helmet at all under the bouffant. Each crew wears
its own overalls, shoulder stripe and helmet colour, and the map reader
beside them is in the same gear, head down over the road book.
