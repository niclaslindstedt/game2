title: A circuit's start straight is no longer buried under a phantom run-off apron
---

The road mesh extrapolated a level dirt apron past both ends of every stage.
On a circuit the ends are the start line, so the finish's run-off was laid
straight over the first fifty metres of the lap and the start's run-up over
the last, at the line's height while the real road climbed or fell
underneath — a car driving the true road was drawn buried to its windows,
and the camera behind it saw nothing but earth. A circuit now draws no
aprons: the road either side of its line is the lap itself.
