title: The benchmark's scene breakdown names what is inside the world
---

The first real report came back saying `world — 438 objects, 924,916
triangles`: two thirds of everything drawable and 87% of the geometry, in
one row that says nothing about which half of it to go after.

The world's subsystems are named now — terrain, wild, cones, breakage,
trains, livestock and the streamed road chunks — so the breakdown says
where the triangles actually are.
