title: Wipers, and glass worth wiping
---

The windscreen and the backlight now get dirty. Rain films them over in
seconds; a gravel stage cakes them slowly and brown, and the rear window
takes the worst of it, the way the wake makes it the window a rally ends
caked.

So both screens have wipers, and they are not decoration: a tandem pair on
the windscreen and a single arm on the backlight, running on demand — fast
in a downpour, an intermittent flick when there is barely anything on the
glass, and always finishing a stroke, so they park where they started. Each
blade clears only the arc it actually sweeps, leaving the corners it never
reaches dirty and a faint smear behind, and the cleared band greys over
again while the blade is away.

The tail lamps' red glow is halved. It read as a lamp from a hundred metres
and as a smear over the whole tail at the range a fight is actually decided
at.

Birds fly on two wings again. Both wings now beat the same way, deep on the
downstroke and shallow on the recovery, instead of tilting opposite ways —
which had been drawing one straight line turning about its middle. The wings
are swept rather than square, so a distant speck reads as a bird.
