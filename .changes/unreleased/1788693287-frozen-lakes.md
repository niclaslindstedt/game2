title: Frozen lakes, and rally roads across them
---

At -5 °C and colder the standing water freezes SOLID — asked of each body's own surface, so a tarn up on a shoulder goes over while the lake in the valley below it is still open. A frozen lake is drawn as bare ice rather than water, is a floor rather than something to drown in, and the stage generator will now route a rally straight out across one: a long, flat, flat-out ice section with the least grip in the game, and no corner tighter than a fast sweeper allowed anywhere near it.
