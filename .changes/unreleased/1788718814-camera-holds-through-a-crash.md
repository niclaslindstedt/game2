---
type: Changed
title: The camera keeps your view through a crash
---

Going over no longer takes the camera away from you and stands it in the
grass. Whichever view you were driving in keeps the frame, holding the angle
it had the moment the car went over: it follows the car through the accident
from that same side, the horizon stays level while the world turns over, and
it only picks the car's direction back up once you are driving again. The
three in-car views are unchanged — they are bolted to the body and still go
right over with it.
