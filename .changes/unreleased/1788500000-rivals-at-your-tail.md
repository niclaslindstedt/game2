type: Changed
title: The rivals actually race you
---

The campaign field used to finish a minute and a half behind a decent lap
even on HARD, which is not a difficulty — it is being alone on the road with
a timing screen. The rivals now drive the pedals like pedals rather than
switches, squeezing the throttle up to a corner and breathing it through a
slide instead of snapping between flat out and nothing, and the settled car
lets the quick crews lean on all of the tire. EASY is a comfortable win with
the leader in sight, MEDIUM is a fight, and on HARD a good drive is only just
enough.
