title: The desert — a second country, six stages, sand under the wheels
---

The campaign goes on past the taiga. Win its table and the DESERT opens: six
more stages — four sprints up the length bands and two circuits — set in a
country that is nothing like the first one. Saguaro stands and creosote
flats on the gravel fans, Joshua trees on the high ground, dune fields the
road rides as a run of blind crests, salt pans where a lake would be if
there were any water, and there is none: no ford, no bridge, no river, no
lake, anywhere.

The road is bladed out of sand rather than stone, and sand is its own
surface: it holds a fifth less than gravel, has to be pushed further
sideways before it bites, and drags at the car the whole way — slower in a
straight line, sideways sooner in every corner, and a slide that runs
further and settles later. The sky is dry too. It does not rain in the
desert; its storm is a dust storm, a wall of blown sand the colour of the
ground with the sun a pale coin in it, and its bad-weather stages are run
in that. Vultures circle where the taiga has crows, nothing crosses the sky
overhead, and lizards bolt off the sand as the car comes past.

Roam offers the country as a dial beside the others (COUNTRY, first in the
STAGE pane), the map viewer opens the desert's stages like the taiga's, the
debug overlay's repro line carries it, and every preview and sim tool takes
`--biome desert`.
