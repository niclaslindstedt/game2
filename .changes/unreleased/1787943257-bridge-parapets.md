title: Bridges are built, and their walls are real
---

A concrete bridge's parapet is now SOLID — put a wheel wide on a deck and
you stop against the wall instead of sailing through it into the river. And
the bridge is held up: its piers and both abutments are founded on the
ravine floor under them rather than on a nominal water level, so it no
longer reads as a slab with one leg somewhere near the middle.
