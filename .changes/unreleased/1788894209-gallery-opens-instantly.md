title: The gallery opens on the press
---

GALLERY used to freeze the menu for a moment before it appeared, because the
filmstrip handed every picture on the roll to the browser at full
1920-by-1080 resolution just to draw it eighty pixels wide — forty
two-megapixel decodes on the frame the row was pressed. The card now goes up
immediately and the pictures arrive behind it: the roll is read off disk
after the page is on screen, and each tile is shrunk to thumbnail size off
the main thread, one at a time, and only once it has been scrolled near.
