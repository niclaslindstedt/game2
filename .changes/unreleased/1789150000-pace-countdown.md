title: The co-driver calls on a clock, with a countdown under the sign
---

The corner call now goes up exactly two seconds before the turn-in — two
seconds at the speed the car is actually doing, so the same sign appears
forty metres out at 70 km/h and a hundred and twenty at 200 — instead of on
a distance window that was a long look at low speed and no warning at all at
high. The second corner is drawn faint underneath when it too lands inside
four seconds, which is a combination you are committing to on the way into
the first rather than one that merely followed close behind it.

Under each sign there is now a yellow countdown bar: full width when the call
goes up, half a second out, nothing left at the turn-in — and it runs out
toward the side the corner turns to, so a right-hander's bar empties to the
right, into the plate's own point. It only ever runs down, and it stops dead
when the car does.
