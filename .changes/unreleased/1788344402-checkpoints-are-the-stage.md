type: Changed
title: Every split board has to be driven through
---

A stage is now the whole of its split boards, in order. Each board is a gate
across the road: drive through it and it books your split, cut across country
around it and it stays owed — and the finish line will not end the run (or, on
a circuit, book the lap) until every board is behind you. Cross the line owing
one and the game says which, and the way-home button puts you back at the last
board you did take.

The minimap marks the board you still owe as a pulsing ring on the route, which
is also the thing to steer at when you have put the car in a field and want to
know which way is back.
