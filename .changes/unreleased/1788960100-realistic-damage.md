type: Changed
title: Damage you can feel, and an engine that really is dead
---

Every panel now costs the air it lets in — a car with no windscreen will not
pull top gear — the headlamps break one at a time, a nose-on hit holes the
radiator and starts a temperature you have to lift for, and `ENGINE DEAD` is
now only said of an engine that has genuinely stopped, which cannot even
reverse the car out of the trees.
