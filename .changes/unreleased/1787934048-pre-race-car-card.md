type: Added
title: Pick your car on its own screen, with the numbers
---

Choosing a stage in the campaign or the time trial now opens a pre-race card
instead of starting the run. The car turns on its stand with its spec sheet
beside it — top speed, 0–100, mass and gears, then bars for acceleration,
traction, braking, tarmac and gravel grip, turn-in and slide, all compared
across the roster — and the transmission, auto or manual, is chosen right
there rather than four screens away in Options. The stage lists lost the car
row that used to sit under them, so a location's whole ladder and its
championship panel fit on screen at once.
