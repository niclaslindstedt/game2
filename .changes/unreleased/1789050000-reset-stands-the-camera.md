title: The camera is stood at the last board on a reset instead of flying round to it
---

Resetting to the last split board after a crash no longer swings the camera round the car. A respawn puts the car down somewhere else on the road facing down the stage, and every reading the shot carried — the boom's yaw and swing, the neck's gaze, the ground under the lens, the verge lens planted for the accident — belonged to where the car was, so all of it was eased across the gap and the press cost about a second of the camera flying round to find the stage again. It is dropped now: the shot is stood where the car has been put in the frame the press cost, in every view.
