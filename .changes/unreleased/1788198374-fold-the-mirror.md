title: Tap the mirror to fold it away
---

The rear-view glass is now its own switch: click or tap it mid-stage and it
folds flat against the top edge of the frame, taking the game's most expensive
render pass with it. Press that strip — or swipe it down — to pull the mirror
back. The fold
lasts for the session and only ever hides the picture — OPTIONS ▸ HUD ▸ REAR
VIEW is still the switch that takes the mirror off for good, and turning it
back on there unfolds the glass with it.
