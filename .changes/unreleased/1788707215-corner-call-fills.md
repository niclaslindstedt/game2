title: The corner call fills as the corner is driven
---

The co-driver's sign has lost the countdown bar under it. Instead the drawn corner lights up in its own colour as the bend goes by — the road first, then the arrow's head filling from its base to its point — standing whole as the car straightens up, and gone the moment the corner is behind it. The signs are also drawn at their proper weight again: the chunky rounded stroke they were designed with had never reached the screen.
