title: The corner call fills as the corner is driven
---

The co-driver's sign has lost the countdown bar under it. Instead the drawn corner writes itself in as the bend goes by — inking from the approach to the head, solid at the exit, and gone the moment the corner is behind the car. The signs are also drawn at their proper weight again: the chunky rounded stroke they were designed with had never reached the screen.
