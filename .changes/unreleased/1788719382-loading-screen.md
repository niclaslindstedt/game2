type: Added
title: A loading screen, and a race that is ready when it lifts
---

Pressing START used to freeze the page for a few seconds and then drop you
into a stage that stuttered for the first minute while the rival crews' runs
were still being worked out behind the racing.

Now there is a loading card — the app's own skid marks, laid from nothing to
full and again — and everything happens behind it: the road is compiled, the
country and its forest are built, every shader the stage needs is compiled,
the fourteen crews are entered, and all fourteen drive the whole stage before
your lights run. When the card lifts there is nothing left to pay for. A
restart loads the same way.

The mark shows up in two more places while you are there: laid once under
SCANDINAVIAN FLICK on the boot screen, and again beside the name on the main
menu.
