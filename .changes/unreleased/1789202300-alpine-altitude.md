title: Alpine altitude slider
---

Roam's alpine country has an ALTITUDE slider: how high the race is, from a
176 m shoulder to a mountain topping out at 6,000 m. The higher it goes the
sheerer the mountain stands, the more of it is above the treeline and the
snowline, and the further there is to fall — the stage is laid along a ledge
across the summit, the ground starts dropping a couple of hundred metres off
the road, and the valley floor with its lakes and its green is still down at
sea level however far up you started.
