title: Grime that vanished when you crashed
---

A stage's dirt no longer disappears off the car at the first knock and
reappear a few hundred metres later. The damage model and the dirt painter
were re-deriving the same panels from their own clean copies of the paint,
so each one wiped the other's work; they now stack, and a car comes out of
an accident bent, scuffed AND as filthy as it went in.
