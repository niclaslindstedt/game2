type: Changed
title: The manual gearbox is worth taking
---

Picking MANUAL now changes the car rather than just who pulls the lever: the
manual is the racing set, with gears 6% taller and 5% more of the engine
reaching the road — about **+6% top speed in any car** — paid for with a beat
of throttle at every shift you now have to take yourself. The pre-race card
quotes both figures through the box you have selected, so flipping the
transmission moves the top speed and the 0–100 on the same screen.

The field drives it too: the campaign's crews with the best hands take their
own gears, so nobody does on EASY, two do on MEDIUM, and six of the fourteen
do on HARD.
