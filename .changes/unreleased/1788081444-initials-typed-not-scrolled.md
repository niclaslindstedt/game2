type: Changed
title: Type your initials on a phone instead of scrolling to them
---

The high score card no longer opens on AAA with a pair of chevrons over every
letter. It opens on ONE letter, and tapping a slot raises the device's own
keyboard — a phone, a tablet and a handheld all have one, so typing your name
there is now typing your name, not walking to it a letter at a time.

Both ways in got shorter. Typed, a letter lands and the next slot stays empty:
NLM is three presses. Wheeled — up and down on a pad or the arrow keys, which
is still the whole entry on anything without a keyboard — an empty slot wakes
holding the letter to its left, so the second and third letters of a name
start next door to where they are going rather than back at A.

A name can now be one or two letters. It used to be padded out with the
letters nobody typed, which is how AAA got onto so many boards.
