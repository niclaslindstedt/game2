title: Rebuilt cars, a third one, and dirt that looks thrown
---

The car generator grew most of a real car. Wheel arches are now cut into
the flanks with a wheel well behind them, panel shut lines are grooves in
the bodywork, and the bonnet and boot lid are separate panels over a dark
bay — so a hard enough nose-first hit now tears the bonnet off and leaves
the engine bay showing. New parts throughout: recessed grilles with a
colored surround, round or rectangular lamps in chrome bezels, bumpers
that wrap the corners, air dams, roof gutters, wipers, plastic arch
extensions, rubbing strips, door handles, mud flaps, number plates,
exhausts, livery bands that arc over the arches, and blocky racing numbers
on the doors. Three rim styles, and box flares for the cars that want them.

Three cars ship now, one glance apart: the Vireo GT is a short upright
hatchback, the Sable 4WD a long three-box race sedan under a red roof, and
the new **Kestrel RS** a wide-arched red rally coupe on quad lamps — light,
grippy and short-geared. Every car also names its drivetrain.

Dirt is spatter instead of a wash: flecks in half a dozen shades of brown,
scattered thickest where the wheels throw them, so the tyres, rims, arches
and sills cake first while the roof only ever dusts.
