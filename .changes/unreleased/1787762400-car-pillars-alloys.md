type: Changed
title: Pillars and alloy wheels on the cars
---

The cabins are built as solid bodywork with the windows cut into them, so
every car now has real A-, B- and C-pillars, a window sill and a roof
header framing its glass instead of one uninterrupted canopy. The hatch
wears thick upright posts and a kicked-up rear quarter light; the coupe a
long door glass ahead of a heavy fastback C-pillar. Wheels gained spoked
alloys — a rim lip, straight-sided spokes and a centre cap over the dark
tyre face — and the mud flaps now hang off the arch instead of floating
beside it.
