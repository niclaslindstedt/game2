title: The taiga is a forest again
---

The taiga's treeline and rock line were set at heights its own country
routinely stands above, so any stage that ran over high ground came out as
bare grey bedrock with no soil and half its trees. The first level of the
game measured 98% rock and 96% above the treeline — which is what the
country's menu banner was a photograph of. The bands now sit at the same
percentiles of the taiga's ground that the desert's sit at of its own, and
the country is green with rock only on the tops.
