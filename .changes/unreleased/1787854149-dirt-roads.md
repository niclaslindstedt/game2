title: Dirt roads look like dirt roads
---

A gravel road now reads as five lines down its length: a loose pale edge on
each side that nothing drives on, two worn wheel tracks a car's width apart,
and the crown between them. The tracks are real troughs, so the road is
visibly curved across its width instead of a flat brown ribbon, and the car
settles into one and has to be steered out. The road's edges fade into the
grass rather than stopping at a ruled line.
