---
type: Changed
title: The time trial opens a whole country at a time instead of one stage at a time
---

A stage used to open in the time trial only once you had personally driven that stage to the finish line in the campaign, which meant the clock arrived stage by stage behind the campaign's own ladder — and putting a time on the sixth road of a country meant podiuming your way down five rungs first. It is a country now: open a location in the campaign and all six of its roads open to the clock together, in any order. The taiga is therefore open from the first time you start the game, and topping the taiga's table hands you the desert's six at once. It is the arrangement a kart game's cups have always used, and it only ever skips the ladder INSIDE a country — the desert is still behind the taiga's table here exactly as it is in the campaign, and a country you had already reached stays open even if you tear its points up. Heads up is unchanged: it still asks for a road you have driven to the end. The country list counts stages TIMED rather than stages open, since every stage of an open country now is.
