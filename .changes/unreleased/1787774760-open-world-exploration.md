type: Added
title: Open-world exploration
---

The landscape is now a place to drive, Forza Horizon style: leave the road
at speed (up to ~150 km/h in the wild; ~230 km/h flat out on the road) and
explore forests, mountains, streams, escarpment cliffs that launch the car,
and the open sea. Boulders and fallen trunks are really there — hit one at
pace, or plunge into deep water, and you crash back onto the track. There
is no off-road timer anymore; the B key (or the HUD's TRACK button) is the
way home.
