title: An arcade attract screen

The opening card now plays like an arcade cabinet. The studio card holds
while the game loads, and the moment it is standing, two crossed finish
flags come up flapping over SCANDINAVIAN FLICK and the screen blinks PRESS
ANY KEY TO START — TAP TO START on a phone. It waits for you instead of
lifting on a timer, and the press that enters is also what lets the browser
turn the sound on.
