type: Changed
title: Stages are scored, and the ground they are laid on comes in layers
---

The stage generator now builds its country in layers — bedrock, the
groundwater in it, and the soil on top — with a per-seed smoothness that says
how long the ice sat on it, so one stage is planed into Swedish whalebacks and
the next keeps its Norwegian crests. Trees only grow where there is soil to
root in, and loose stone only surfaces where the cover is thin.

Several things that were quietly wrong are fixed with it: rivers now always
end somewhere — a lake, the edge of the map, or a tarn they fill themselves —
instead of stopping in a field, and they widen as they run instead of
occasionally narrowing. Branch roads no longer cross each other or fold back
over their own line. Jumps are shallower and more varied, so the big ones read
as big instead of every one being a moon shot. And the start line now has room
for a full sixteen-car heads-up grid, with a straight off the line long enough
for the field to string out on before the first corner.
