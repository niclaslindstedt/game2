title: Every rival crew has colours of their own
---

The campaign's fourteen crews are no longer painted by their place on the
entry list — each has a scheme authored to fit how they drive, and a colour
nobody else in the field has. Sideways wears the drifter's crescent in
purple and acid green. Granite is cut stone, blocked front and rear with the
door left bare. Old Snow runs maroon with gold coachlines, the oldest-looking
car in the paddock. Skarv is a cormorant — dark back, pale belly. And the
door roundel is the crew's start number, because this is a real start list.
