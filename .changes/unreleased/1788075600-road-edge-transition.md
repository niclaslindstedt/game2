title: The gravel road meets the grass
---

The road's edge is a transition rather than a boundary now. Loose stone
lies on the mat itself — everywhere except the two lines every car before
you drove down — and runs off it into the grass, thinning away over ten
metres instead of stopping. Grass grows back the other way, over the loose
margin the wheels never touch. And the country beside a gravel road wears
the dust off it, fading out over nine metres, so the ground does not go
back to full meadow green in the single step where the road mesh ends.
