title: The road under the car
---

Leaving the road and coming back no longer throws the car into the air. The
run was looking up the road under the wheels from how far it had GOT rather
than from where the car actually was, so a car that had been up a mountain or
turned round was handed the height of road it was only in line with — fifty
metres of teleport on level 1, then a drop into the lake. Along with it: a
hill now pushes back the same way whichever way the car is pointed (driving
back down a stage used to be uphill both ways), a jump throws a car that
comes at it the other way round instead of crashing it, and the rocks, trees
and grass beside the road stand on the ground you can see rather than hanging
several metres over it.
