type: Changed
title: Rally glass — one wiper, a clean fan, and a back window worth looking through
---

The cars wear a single centre wiper on each screen now, the way a stage car
does, instead of a road car's tandem pair on the windscreen. Each one parks
flat on the sill and sweeps a half circle just short of the header, so what
a dirty screen wears is one big clean fan with all four corners still caked.

The blades also stop running the whole time. Rain starts them at the first
hint of it and they keep going while it falls; a dry stage's dust has to
build up properly before it is worth a stroke, and then it gets one and a
long wait. They move at something like a real wiper's pace, roughly twice
what they did.

What settles on the glass is the right colour at last: dry road grime is
pale sand rather than the dark brown of wet mud, and it goes on heavily
enough that the fan the blade cuts through it reads from behind — which is
where the player is looking.

The back windows are wider on every car, filling most of the panel instead
of sitting between two hands' breadths of paint.

Fixed: the dashboard and the instrument hood were built out over the
scuttle on shorter cabins, showing from outside as a dark bar lying across
the bottom of the windscreen — over the glass, the grime on it and the
parked wiper under that.
