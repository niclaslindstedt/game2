title: The wheel is in front of the dials again
---

From the driver's seat, two of the three cars had their steering wheel drawn
INSIDE the dashboard — the dials sat on top of the rim and the top of the
wheel poked out through the fascia. Every car now gets a rim standing clear
in front of its own instruments, and the dials are smaller, sitting inside
the wheel's opening with air round them instead of filling it corner to
corner. The in-car lens is ten degrees tighter in all three views, so the
cabin frames the road rather than the road swallowing the cabin. SEAT ▸ TALL
and FIELD OF VIEW ▸ ULTRA are gone: both were past the point where the
header rail comes down over the apex of every corner.
