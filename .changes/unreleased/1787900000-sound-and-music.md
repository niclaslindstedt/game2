title: The game has sound
---

Scandinavian Flick makes a noise now, and none of it is a file: every effect
and every note is synthesized in the browser.

- **The car.** A four-cylinder engine whose pitch is the revs and whose grit is
  the load, the tyres reading whatever surface is under them (graded gravel,
  tarmac, forest floor, water), wind that opens up with speed, and — the one
  the game is about — a drift that digs on gravel and sings on tarmac, and gets
  louder and wider the more sideways the car actually is.
- **The stage.** Countdown lights, the off, gear changes, fords, jumps and
  landings, the road running out under the wheels, contacts from a brush past a branch to a head-on that folds a wing,
  parts tearing off, the wreck, the respawn, and the finish.
- **The menus.** A click for every row, a switch for every option, a whoosh for
  every page, and one big noise for the launch onto a stage.
- **Two scores.** STARTING RAMP under the menu and TAIGA, FLAT OUT
  on the stage, both looping tracker arrangements written for this game.
- **Options → Audio.** Separate faders for the effects and the music, in five
  steps each, OFF included.
