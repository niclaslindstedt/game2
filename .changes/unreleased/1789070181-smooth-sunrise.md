type: Fixed
title: The sunrise arrives as a sunrise
---

A stage driven through the dawn — First Light in the Alps most of all —
used to light up in visible jumps: the mist in the valley changed tone a
tenth at a time, and the mountain's shadow came down the far wall in steps
instead of sliding. The sun's own place is now read every frame rather than
four times a second, and the shadow the country throws is held at two
moments and read between them, so the light comes up as one continuous
movement.
