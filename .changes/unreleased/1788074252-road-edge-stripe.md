title: The stripe down the side of every road
---

A dirt-coloured line ran the length of every stage a few metres out in the
grass, and on some ground it was a wall metres high. The road's skirt — the
curtain that closes the gap under a raised road — was hung from the road's
own height instead of from the vertex the road actually ends at, so it stood
proud of the country it was supposed to be buried in. The gravel now runs
out into the grass with nothing drawn at the join.
