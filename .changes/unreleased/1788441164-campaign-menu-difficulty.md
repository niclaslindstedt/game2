---
type: Changed
title: The campaign menu's standings and difficulty
---

The location page gives its whole height back to the stage boxes: the standings panel under them is now a STANDINGS button at the top of the page, and where you stand, what you have scored, the gate on the next country and the reset all live in the table it opens. The stage the campaign would pick up at wears a ring instead of a CONTINUE button.

Difficulty is now three cards rather than three chips, marked EASY, MEDIUM and HARD in green, amber and red with a meter that says how far up the ladder each one stands and what a crash costs there — and it is called DIFFICULTY, because it sets how good the field is and how much of every impact lands on your own car.
