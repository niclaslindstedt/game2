type: Changed
title: The campaign menu's standings and difficulty
---

The location page gives its whole height back to the stage boxes. The standings panel under them is gone: STANDINGS at the top of the page opens the location's board instead — the same board the results card shows at the end of a stage, asked what all six of them added up to, with every crew in order beside a picture of their car, the wins that break a tie, and the reset. The stage the campaign would pick up at wears a ring on the grid where a CONTINUE button used to sit.

RIVALS is now DIFFICULTY, and three chips are three cards: a meter that says how far up the ladder each rung stands, in green, amber and red, and under the word the two things it decides — how good the fourteen crews are, and how much of every crash your own car keeps. HEADS UP asks the same question, about its own field, with the same cards.
