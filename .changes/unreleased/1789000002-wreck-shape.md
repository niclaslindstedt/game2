title: Wrecked cars fold like sheet metal
---

A crashed car now folds back from the face it hit, wraps a V round a trunk it slid into sideways, tents its bonnet, creases and chips its paint where the metal moved, bends its nose toward the corner that took the hit and sits down on a flat tyre, instead of shattering into a burst of white splinters — and every part it loses lands on the ground instead of hanging in the air.
