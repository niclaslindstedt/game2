title: DETAIL decides whose car can lose a wheel
---

A shed wheel is not free: a hub mesh and a material per corner, a body
sitting crooked on a fitted plane, and — where EFFECTS allows it — a whole
extra rigid body rolling down the stage with its own contacts against the
ground, the car and everything standing in the way. Fifteen cars trading
paint can serve several of those at once.

So it joins the DETAIL row, on the split the dust, the exhaust and the
folded panels already use. **HIGH** lets anybody be put out. **MEDIUM**
sheds only from the car being driven — the one whose lost wheel is a fact
the driver has to get home on — and leaves the field's wheels bolted on.
**LOW** keeps every wheel on every car.

A car whose wheels stick still handles like the three-wheeler the ledger
says it is; nothing in the engine changes. And the row only decides whether
a wheel may LEAVE: one already gone has gone, and turning the row back up
sheds what was already lost without throwing it — nothing flies off a car
for a wheel it lost a corner ago.
