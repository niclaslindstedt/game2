title: Every car has real tailpipes, and the works sedan has two
---

The cars now carry a silencer and a length of pipe under the floorpan, and the exhaust comes out of it: smoke leaves the pipe the bodywork actually has, on the side it has it. The four-wheel-drive works sedan runs a twin exit and puts up a plume off each. And the pipe is the lowest thing on the car, so the ground can take it — land a jump flat and hard enough and the whole assembly tears off, tumbles down the road behind you, and the rest of the stage is driven trailing black smoke out of the break.
