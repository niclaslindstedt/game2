title: Pausing and quitting actually silence the run
---

The pause card stopped the music but left the car making noise: the engine,
the tyres, the wind and the country carried on behind it, and walking out to
the main menu left the same hum playing under the menu theme. The beds are
now hushed on every frame nothing is hearing the run — behind the card, under
god mode's held camera, on the Roam map and behind every menu — and the note
picks back up where it left off on RESUME.
