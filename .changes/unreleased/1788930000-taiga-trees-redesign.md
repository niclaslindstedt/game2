title: The taiga's trees stand at their real height
---

The boreal forest's spruces, pines, firs, larches and birches are redrawn at
twenty to thirty metres with a rare old-growth giant near fifty among them,
eleven new species join the roster, and every bough, fork and cactus joint —
the crooked pine's upper trunk, the bog pine, the oak, the willow, the cholla,
the yucca, the mesquite — now sits on the trunk that grew it instead of
floating beside it.
