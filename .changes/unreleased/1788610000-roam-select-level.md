type: Added
title: Pick a campaign stage in Roam — and drive it
---

Roam's map now has a SELECT LEVEL button: choose one of the campaign's own
stages and it lands on the map with its seed, its length and the conditions
it was authored in already set, ready to be changed — another hour, worse
weather, a different car, another shape of road — and driven. The header
names whichever stage is on the map, and stops naming one the moment you
step the seed or move a dial onto a road the campaign never shipped.

Blowing the map up to full screen no longer takes DRIVE IT away with the
rest of the panels, so the stage you are looking at is always one press from
being the stage you are driving.
