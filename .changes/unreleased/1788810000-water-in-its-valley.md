title: Water lies in its valley — fords dip to the river, streams run under the road in culverts, and the taiga's roads sit on the country
---

A river crossing the road used to leave its water on the mat a metre above
the river beside it, a sheet standing on nothing where the road ran over a
fill. Water runs downhill: a ford now lies where the valley is, the road
dipping down to the river's own level over a graded apron each side and
climbing back out, and the pool it crosses is held at that one level for a
stretch either side so the river you drive through is the river you saw
coming. Where the road stands too high over a small stream to dip to it at
all, the stream goes UNDER the road instead, through a round concrete pipe
in the fill with a headwall each end — the water enters one side and comes
out the other at the level it arrived at, and the road never gets wet.

The taiga's stages are built to the desert's standard now. The generator
plans each road in height as well as on the map, so a road no longer meets
its own earlier arm across a hillside it would have to cut a cliff through,
a jump lands on a road that is there to land on, a bridge's deck and the
water under it agree, and the road's outer band grades down to the country
instead of stepping off it. On the twelve seeds the analyzer scores, the
taiga went from a hundred and six errors to three and the desert from nine
to three.
