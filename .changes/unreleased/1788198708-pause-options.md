type: Added
title: Options from the pause card
---

Pausing mid-stage now opens OPTIONS without leaving the run. It is the same
page the main menu offers — HUD, audio, video and controls — and its way out
goes back to the pause card rather than out to the menu, so the camera you
cannot see out of or the effects level that corner just cost you is a change
you make and drive on from. A camera picked there lands on the car at once
instead of at the next start line.
