title: Fourteen rivals in the campaign, and a podium to make
---

Every campaign stage is now a real rally: fifteen crews leave the start
control ten seconds apart and you are the last car on the road. The other
fourteen are not a table of times — they drive the same stage at the same
moment, in their own cars, on the real bot, and lose the seconds anybody
loses. Your **position** rides in the top right beside the map and moves at
every split board, and the split names the crew whose board time you are
measured against.

Finish in the **top three** and the stage is cleared and the next one opens.
Finish outside it and the card at the line says TOO SLOW, with the stage
there to run again. **RIVALS** on the stage list picks how good the field is
— easy, medium or hard — and every stage box remembers your best finishing
position at the setting you are looking at.

Time trial now opens on any stage you have driven to the finish line rather
than only the ones you podiumed on: a time is something you chase on a road
you have already seen the end of.
