---
type: Changed
title: Roam rebuilt around the map and the settings
---

Roam is now the map and one column of settings, and nothing else. Every
setting is the row the Options page is built from — a mark, a name, and the
value between two arrows — grouped as STAGE, SKY and LAND, so a page of
fourteen choices is scanned instead of read. The seed's own number is a
press that rolls a new one.

SELECT A LEVEL puts one of the campaign's roads on the map through the
campaign's OWN stage boxes, route drawings and all, rather than a list of
seed numbers.

The car moved off the page and onto the pre-race card AFTER it — the same
card the campaign, the time trial and heads up hand off to — so a road and a
car are two decisions again.

The generator's layer switch and the full-screen map came off Roam
altogether: they live on DEVELOPER → MAP VIEWER, which is the page for
reading a stage rather than choosing one.
