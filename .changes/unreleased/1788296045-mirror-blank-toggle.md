---
type: Changed
title: The rear-view mirror goes grey instead of folding away
---

Tapping the rear-view mirror mid-stage now leaves it exactly where it hangs and simply switches the picture off — flat grey glass with REAR VIEW MIRROR OFF floating on it — instead of folding the whole thing into a thin strip at the top of the frame. Tap the same grey to bring the road behind back, and it says REAR VIEW MIRROR ON over the returning picture before fading. Switching it on is now the same tap in the same place as switching it off, which is the whole difference on a phone.
