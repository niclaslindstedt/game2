---
type: Changed
title: Touch wheel has weight, and shows its lock
---

The phone steering wheel no longer snaps to your thumb: it turns toward it at a rate set by how far you have pushed, so a small drag is a small correction instead of an instant slide, and only a committed shove reaches full lock. A blue arc fills the rim from 12 o'clock round to the marker, so how much steering the car actually has is readable mid-drift.
