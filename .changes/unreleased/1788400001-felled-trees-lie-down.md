type: Fixed
title: A tree the car knocks down now falls over
---

A trunk snapped by the car used to sink straight into the ground where it stood and settle at whatever angle it happened to be at, leaving bare poles growing out of the hillside and logs hanging in mid-air behind a run through the forest. It now goes over the way it was pushed, lands as a splintered bole with the stubs of its lowest boughs on it, and comes to rest lying flat in the grass.
