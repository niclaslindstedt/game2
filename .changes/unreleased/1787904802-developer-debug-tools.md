type: Added
title: God mode and the debug overlay
---

The developer menu (seven taps on the car) gained two tools, also on the
pause card mid-run. **God mode** takes the camera off the car and flies it —
WASD with strafing, space up, ctrl down, shift for speed, mouse or arrows to
look — and holds the run still for as long as you are up there, so the moment
you flew out to look at is the moment you land back into. **Debug overlay** puts boxes in
the corner naming the stage, where the camera is standing, and what the car
is doing, and along the bottom a REPRO line: a URL that reopens that exact
frame on anyone else's machine. Holding **Alt** hides the game's HUD and
leaves those boxes up, so a screenshot can show the game and still say where
it was taken. **DEVELOPER → DEBUG LOG** keeps what led there — every engine
event and a position line a second — copyable whole or for the latest run.
