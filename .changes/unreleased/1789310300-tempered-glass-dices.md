title: Tempered glass shatters instead of sailing away
---

A window leaving the car used to fly off as one intact sheet, turning over
like a page — which is not what glass does, and read as a cartoon in the
middle of a crash. Now only the windscreen leaves in one piece: it is
laminated, and the plastic between its two sheets is what holds it
together, so it comes out folded and falls. Every other window on the car
is tempered, which is gravel the moment it lets go anywhere — it leaves as
a burst out of the frame and nothing else, and how much of it there is and
how far it opens is how hard the pane was let go of.
