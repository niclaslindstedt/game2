title: Dust that knows where the light is
---

The clouds a car raises are now lit rather than fullbright. Head lamps throw
a warm cone into anything hanging in front of the car, tail lamps turn the
cloud directly behind it red — which is the part the chase camera looks
straight through — and with the lamps off a night plume all but disappears
instead of glowing the same tan it does at noon. The daylight cloud is
unchanged.

The whole field raises dust now, not just the car being driven: every crew
on the road tows a plume of its own, lit by its own lamps, and starts doing
it further away than their car is even drawn — a rival is a cloud over the
road a corner before it is a car.

The towed cloud is also half as opaque as it was, so the road ahead stays
readable through the thickest part of the tail.
