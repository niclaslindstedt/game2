---
type: Changed
title: Every country's ladder rebuilt, and its circuits moved into the climb
---

All eighteen campaign stages were re-picked. Each country now runs a sprint, a
circuit, a sprint, a circuit, a sprint and the long one — so the three-lap races
are part of the climb instead of two short rings tacked on after the finale, and
every stage asks more than the one before it. Each country also runs all four
seasons, every hour band and every weather its own sky has, and there is a stage
in each for every car in the garage.
