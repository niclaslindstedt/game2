type: Changed
title: The four-wheel-drive's nose rounds down to its real lip, and the roadside stone bar follows it
---

The all-wheel-drive sedan's bonnet now rounds down over its last forty centimetres to the 0.74 m lip the elevation has, instead of running flat a hand above it. That made it the lowest nose in the catalog, and the engine's bar for which roadside stones stand as solids is set against exactly that, so the bar comes down with it: a stone taller than 0.43 m (was 0.5 m) is now placed as a solid, and one whose top stands under 0.52 m (was 0.6 m) is ridden over by the wheels rather than met by the body. Slightly more of the small stone beside a stage can be hit, and a stone between half a metre and sixty centimetres tall now meets the bumper instead of passing under it.
