title: The chase camera has weight of its own
---

The boom behind the car now behaves like something with mass on the end of
it: it falls back as the car accelerates away, carries forward over a car
braking under it, and settles onto the rig's own length the moment the car
is doing neither — including at a standstill. It also stands a little
further back the faster the car is going, so pace is felt in the framing
before it is read off the dial.

The camera's lean through a turn or a drift is much subtler than it was —
roughly half through a gripped corner and a third of what it was through a
slide. The drift is displayed by the car swinging across the frame; the
horizon stays where it belongs.
