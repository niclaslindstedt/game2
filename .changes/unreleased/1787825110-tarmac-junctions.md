title: Tarmac sections, and the junctions they join at
---

Stages now borrow public asphalt roads for stretches at a time. Tarmac holds a
third more grip — the corner that needed a slide can be driven round, and the
drifts that do happen smoke tires instead of throwing gravel. The route joins
and leaves the tarmac at proper junctions: the arm it does not take carries
straight on, taped off with cones and a chevron board, and it is real road —
follow it if you want to see where it goes.
