title: The camera keeps the road in the shot through a deep drift
---

The chase cameras carried the drift's whole slip angle in a straight line,
so a big slide laid the car across the frame and the road behind it — and
because the three drivetrains reach very different angles on purpose, the
rear- and four-wheel-drive cars got a far more sideways picture than the
front-driver for the same corner. The framing now eases onto a ceiling
instead: an ordinary slide is carried exactly as sideways as it is, and the
deepest ones settle at about half the angle they used to, within a couple of
degrees of each other whichever car is driving. The cars still drive
completely differently; they are just filmed the same. From above (`heli`
and `top`) and in the air, nothing is capped — there the drift blocks
nothing and the shot's job is the landing.
