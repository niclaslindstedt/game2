type: Changed
title: Steadier cameras, and CLOSE by default
---

The chase view now carries the same steadying numbers as the close one — the
same follow rate, the same swing spring, the same lift and duck over a hill —
so standing further back changes the framing and not how settled the picture
is. The view behind the bumper is the one a new player starts in; the camera
key still walks the whole ladder.

The hood camera is calmer too. The road's buzz comes through the seat as a
vibration a frame can actually draw rather than a different offset every
frame, it grows softly with the surface instead of piling up on the rough
stuff — there are springs in the way — and the driver's neck now eases into
the end of its travel instead of hitting it, so a landing throws the head
without knocking the picture. Uneven ground still moves you around; it no
longer shakes the screen apart.
