title: The rivals race each other, and some of them race dirty
---

The field is no longer fourteen cars driving through one another. Every crew
can see the cars around it — you included — and they are solid to each other:
catch two rivals fighting over the same corner and it is a real fight, with
real bodywork.

What a crew does about the car in front is now part of who they are. A tidy
driver leaves a car's width of air, and sits in behind rather than drive into
the back of anybody. A crew with a temper takes the air out of the gap and
leans on you going past. The worst of them are not passing you at all — they
pick the side of the road that leaves you nowhere to go, and they will put a
wheel into your back corner where the trees start.

Difficulty decides how far any of that goes. Easy is a polite field with two
or three crews who will still arrive in your door; medium leans; hard is where
the rear quarter gets used. Scrapper is the one to watch at every setting.
