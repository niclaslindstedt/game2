title: The car breaks for real — dead engines, lost wheels, shattered glass, doors, and a retirement
---

A crash now costs what a crash costs. A wall met square at 100 km/h kills the
engine outright, and a dead engine is the run: no power, a seized crank that
drags the car to a stop in a few lengths, black smoke off the bonnet, and a
RETIRED card over the car where it stopped — no time, no place, no points, just
the reason and the two ways off (the same stage from the grid, or the menu). At
50 km/h the same hit only hurts the engine, and from the moment it is called
DAMAGED steam comes off the bonnet, thicker and darker the worse it gets. A
rival's engine dies the same way, and files them as a DNF.

Every wheel keeps a ledger of its own now, fed by the corner and the flank it
sits behind and by a landing taken on that side. A tyre goes down first — the
car pulls toward it with the wheel straight, the rim drags, and the drawn wheel
squashes, leans and wobbles once a turn — and then the wheel comes off
altogether: it tumbles away, the corner drops onto its hub, the body sits
crooked for the rest of the run, and the car crawls. Two wheels gone is a car
that cannot be driven, and the same retirement. The brakes are a fifth system
that gives with the corners: the pedal loses part of its bite and the handbrake
nearly all of it, so a car with cut lines cannot be flicked round a hairpin on
the lever.

The glass shatters — windscreen, backlight, each flank's windows — and stays
gone, grime and all, so the cabin is seen straight into. A flank driven hard
into a rock takes the door with it, and what is left is the dark of the cabin
where the door was, stripes and all. The bodywork folds twice as far as it did
for the same hit and buckles rather than scales: a car that has met a wall at
100 km/h has no front. Each thing that gives says so on the screen — BRAKES
DAMAGED, FRONT LEFT PUNCTURE, FRONT LEFT WHEEL LOST, ENGINE DEAD — and each one
has its sound. The difficulty's damage assist scales all of it exactly as it
scaled the dents, so an EASY run cannot be retired by the car.
