title: Opponents in Roam
---

Roam has an OPPONENTS slider: put up to thirty-one other cars on the road
with you and race them off one mass-start grid, on any seed, in any weather.
It defaults to none — Roam is still the road to yourself until you ask for
company — and the top of the travel is a long way past what the game is
balanced for, which is the point: how many cars your machine will draw is
yours to find. The stage lays extra run-up behind the start line to stand the
whole grid on, and the field is the fourteen named crews plus club entries
behind them.
