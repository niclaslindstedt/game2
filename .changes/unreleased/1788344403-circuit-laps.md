title: Circuit laps after the first
---

On a lapped circuit the run's progress used to snap to the end of the road the
moment you crossed the line for a new lap — the start line and the finish line
are the same piece of gravel — and stay pinned there for the whole of that lap.
Every lap after the first was driven with the pacenotes, the crowd, the map's
progress gauge and the rival drivers all reading a car that had already
finished. Laps two and three are now driven as laps.
