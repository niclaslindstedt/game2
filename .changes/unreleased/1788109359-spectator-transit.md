title: The camera flies backwards up the road to the crew behind
---

The move onto a rival's car after your own run ends now reads as a
helicopter reversing up the stage: it backs off the finish line, arcs over
whatever hill is in the way with the lens tilting down over it, and settles
in behind the next crew still coming — in the same view you were just
driving in, pointing the same way it started. It used to whip round to face
the target, tumble as the aim swept through the camera itself, and snap
forward again on the last frame. Long transits are given a little more time
than short ones, changing which view a car is watched from no longer lobs
the camera into the air, and the results card and SPECTATE now share one
shot instead of flying between two.
