type: Added
title: Six camera angles, and a default you can set
---

The camera key (V) now walks six views instead of two, from inside the car
outwards: HOOD, CLOSE behind the bumper, the arcade CHASE, a FAR view stood
back for more warning, a HELI shot flown high and behind, and TOP straight
over the roof and tilted down the road. The distant three have weight —
their swing is sprung, so a turn throws them to the outside of it and they
settle back rather than snapping square behind the car. OPTIONS → HUD picks
which one every stage starts on.
