title: The benchmark's graph no longer collapses on the summary
---

The finished card added a score, four video cells and two buttons to a
column that already had the graph in it — and on a phone held sideways that
is taller than the screen, so the layout took the height back out of the one
item that could give: the graph, which shrank to a thumbnail exactly when
there was finally something worth looking at on it.

It keeps its size now and the card scrolls instead. The card is also wider,
because what it exists to show is a graph and a graph is only as good as the
width it is drawn across — and the graph on a finished run is a **press**:
it opens the whole run full screen, where a hundred and twenty readings are
far enough apart to read.
