type: Fixed
title: Marking stands at corners, and nothing grows out of tarmac
---

The marker posts beside a gravel road no longer stand down empty straights.
Every piece of kerbing is now clipped to the bend it belongs to: the apex
through the middle of it, the turn-in board through the first of a hard one
and the exit board through the last, each ending where the corner does. Only
a hazard — a bridge parapet, a jump lip's shoulders — reaches past a bend,
because what it is wrapped around is not one.

And the verge stops at the tarmac. The grass that closes in over a gravel
road's loose margin was growing out of sealed road and bridge decks too; it
now only ever reaches onto a surface loose enough for it.
