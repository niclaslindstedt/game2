type: Changed
title: Landings have weight now
---

A jump is no longer over when the wheels touch. The springs bottom, the body
is thrown back up off them and the tyres hop — and while that is happening
there is less car standing on the road, so a nose a few degrees off line or a
wheel with any lock on it takes you sideways. The hit is sized by how hard you
came down rather than by how long you were up, so a short drop off a steep lip
lands harder than a long floaty flight, and the camera, the dust and the thump
are all scaled off that. Even the smallest lip on a stage now lands like a car
arriving: heavy, and worth being straight for.
