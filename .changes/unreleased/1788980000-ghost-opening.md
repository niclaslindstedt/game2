title: The time trial's ghost drives again
---

A ghost recorded on a run whose driver pressed on during the establishing
shot replayed the whole shot instead, spending the first seconds of the tape
parked on the line and then driving every corner seconds behind — usually
into the first tree. The cut is now written down as part of the run and taken
again at the same step, so the ghost drives the lap it was recorded on. Old
ghosts cannot be read back and are replaced by the next run on the stage,
whether or not it beats the record.
