title: The desert is empty country — no homesteads, no towns
---

Nobody lives on a desert stage any more. The homesteads off the road and the
towns along the tarmac were the taiga's, painted in the taiga's colours, and
they stood in the desert exactly the same; a desert road runs a hundred
kilometres between one place and the next, so now whether a country is lived
in is a row in its biome, and the desert's says it is not. The railway and the
farms are the taiga's for the same reason.
