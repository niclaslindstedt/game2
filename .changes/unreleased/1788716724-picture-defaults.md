type: Changed
title: The picture the game starts on
---

RESOLUTION is now a share of your device's own screen rather than a fixed ceiling: HIGH draws every pixel the display has — two or three per CSS pixel on a modern phone, where it used to stop at one — and each stop down halves the picture in each direction.

A fresh install now starts on HIGH resolution, MEDIUM detail and NEAR distance: the pixels bought first because a soft picture is what you cannot look harder to fix, the detail left on the number the game was tuned on, and the far ridges given up to pay for both. The rear-view mirror also starts switched off — it is a second pass over the whole stage and the most expensive thing on screen, so it is now something you turn on rather than something a struggling phone has to find and turn off.
