type: Fixed
title: Lakes have a surface again
---

Every lake, ford and stream was wound inside out, so the water sheet was
culled away and what you saw where a lake should be was its bed — ground
painted lake-bottom blue, shimmering as it aliased. The water is drawn now:
one flat semi-transparent sheet per body that you can see the stones
through, with the sun's glitter landing on it where the sun actually is.
