title: Stage dials — hills, water, forest, tarmac
---

Four knobs in the pre-race menu decide what KIND of country a seed builds in:
how hilly, how wet, how forested, and how much of the road is asphalt. They
reach the tooling too (`npm run sim -- --asphalt 0.8`, `npm run track --
--water 1`) and the URL (`?elevation=&water=&trees=&asphalt=`).
