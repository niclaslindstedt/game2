type: Changed
title: Twelve cars on a field, and the grid you choose from
---

Every race but Roam now runs a field of at most **twelve cars**, the player
included — a place out of twelve is a position you can hold in your head at
racing speed, where the deep end of the entry list was only ever names going
past. The campaign's start list is eleven crews spread evenly across the
roster, so the benchmark is still at the head of it and the tail is still at
the back, and a location's table is those twelve and nobody else.

Heads up asks the same question with one chip fewer: **2, 4, 8 or 12**
participants. Roam is untouched — its opponents slider still stands a full
grid of thirty-two on a stage built to hold them.
