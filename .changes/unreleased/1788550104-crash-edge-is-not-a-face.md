title: A car balanced on its edge is not lying down
---

A crash that stopped up on one side — two wheels and the sill on the ground,
the body still leaning — was read as a car lying flat on a face. It got
handed back as if it had come to rest and then retired for being off its
wheels in the same breath, so braking a rollover to a standstill could still
take you back to the last split board. A face has to reach both ways now, and
an edge is what it is: the ridge between two of them.
