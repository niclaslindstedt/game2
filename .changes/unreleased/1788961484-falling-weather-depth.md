title: Rain and snow read at three ranges
---

Both sheets are built out of a near shell and a far one now, so what falls
carries out of the bubble round the car and into the country: long bright
streaks or crystals at the glass, thinner hatching and fine specks over the
middle distance, and each one dissolving at the rim of its own reach instead
of blinking out at a fixed radius. Past that it is the AIR — a squall now
closes the view down as it thickens, and heavy snow whitens the distance to
the flakes' own colour and takes the ridge line with it, so a downpour is
obvious from the far end of the frame rather than only on the windscreen.

The rain is lit by the headlamps as well, off the same register the dust and
the snow already read: a night shower is a cone of bright streaks a few
metres off the bumper — a scatter with dark air between it, where snow in the
same beams is a wall.
