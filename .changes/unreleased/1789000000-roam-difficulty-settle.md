type: Changed
title: Roam's difficulty slider builds the stage once, when you let go
---

Dragging DIFFICULTY on the Roam page used to rebuild the whole stage at
every position the thumb passed through — twenty roads generated, compiled
and stood up in the world on the frames the finger needed to move, which
made the control crawl. The drag is now a draft: the word and the track
follow the thumb the whole way, and the map answers once, on release. The
reading also stands OVER the track now, where every other row on the page
puts its value, instead of beside it.
