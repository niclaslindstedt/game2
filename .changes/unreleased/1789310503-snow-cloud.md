title: A car ploughing deep snow drives in a cloud of its own making
---

Snow thrown up by a car now answers to how deep the snow actually is. Below
the sills it washes off the wheels and trails behind, as it always did. Past
them the body is shouldering the snow aside rather than the wheels running
through it, and what it parts comes up over the nose instead — off the front
of the car, thrown upward, and wider, so a car wading snow up to its bonnet is
looking through the cloud it is making. There is more of it the deeper the
snow gets, and in the deepest fields it breaks clean over the roof.
