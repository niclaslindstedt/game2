title: DETAIL decides whose car wears its dents
---

Folding a body into the shape of what it hit is the most expensive thing a
car does — every vertex on it re-derived and every face lit again, on the
frame of the impact — and a pack of fifteen trading paint off one green pays
that bill several times a second, right where the racing is closest.

So it rides the DETAIL row now, split the way the dust and the exhaust
already are. **HIGH** bends the whole entry list: a rally an hour old is a
field of bent cars. **MEDIUM** bends the one car the damage is news about —
the car being driven, two metres from the camera, whose crush the HUD is
reporting — and leaves the field straight. **LOW** leaves every car clean.

What comes OFF a car is not on this row: a bumper, a door, a pane and a
wheel still leave on their own events at every stop, and a car that has lost
a wheel still sits down on the hub.
