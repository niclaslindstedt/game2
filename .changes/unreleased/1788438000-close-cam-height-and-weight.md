title: A close camera you can see the road from, and less weight in the boom
---

The `close` camera stood too near the ground: from four metres back the car
fills half again as much of the frame as it does from `chase`, so the
look-over angle the whole ladder shares hung its roofline high in the
picture and left barely any road over it. It stands forty centimetres higher
now — about 18° of look-over instead of 13° — which puts its roofline back
where the rest of the ladder puts one and hands the driver back the road
ahead.

The spring the outside cameras carry their height on is stiffer too. It
still refuses a crease, a rut or a kerb — a bump is ridden in the frame
rather than followed — but a real change of level is now most of the way
answered inside a fifth of a second instead of trailing the car up the hill.
