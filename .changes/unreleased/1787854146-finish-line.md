title: The finish is a line on a road, not the end of one
---

Stages now carry on past the finish gate. Cross the line and the clock stops
but the car does not: it coasts on down the run-out road while the camera
plants itself at the gate and pans to watch you go, and the run ends when the
car has actually come to rest. The finish used to be the last two metres of
road in the world.
