title: The dirt on the glass runs to the frame of every window from the driver's seat
---

From the cockpit there was a clear band round every window between the edge of the grime and the pillar, with the landscape showing through it: the rubber seal between the opening and the glass was only ever drawn facing out. The cabin lining now closes that band from inside, so the coat on the windscreen and the side glass reaches the frame. The wiper leaves a ridge of dirt along the bottom of the windscreen too, the way a real blade does, instead of sweeping the sill as clean as the middle.
