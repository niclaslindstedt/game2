type: Fixed
title: Rollovers settle, and the crew get taken back
---

A car that ends a roll off its wheels now comes to rest and the crew are
taken back to the last split board, however it ended up there — on its roof,
on a flank, or nose-over-tail. It could get stuck before: the roll never let
go of a car it had put down, and a car lying upside down with no roll left in
it was left there for the rest of the run.

The ground now decides a crashing car's spin the right way round, so a car
merely sliding on its roof no longer winds itself up to a turn a second, and
a car on its roof on a bank slides down it instead of standing itself back
up. Each car rolls and spins according to its own weight and how that weight
is spread, so the heavy coupe genuinely resists going end over end where the
light hatch does not. The gravel and dust a crash throws are now sized by how
much energy the ground actually took, so a hard hit throws four times what a
hit half as hard does instead of twice. The outside camera lets go the moment
you have caught the car and are driving again, rather than watching you
accelerate away from the verge.
