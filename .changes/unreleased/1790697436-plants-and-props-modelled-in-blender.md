title: Modelled undergrowth and props
---

Everything beside the road that was still built out of boxes is now a
proper model — the grass in arched blades and fern fronds, the moss
cushions, the flowers, the cattails and lily pads, the cacti with rounded
pads and real spines, the cut stumps and lying logs, the passing traffic
with real car bodies and wheels, the train, the farms' tractors and gear,
their cows and sheep, the wind turbines' machines, the solar tables, the
stones, the spectators, and the road's marker posts and cones.
