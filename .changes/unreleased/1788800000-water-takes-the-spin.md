title: Water stops a car that lands in it
---

A car that went into a lake spinning kept spinning — a car arriving off a
jump turned two full circles on the surface before it settled, which read as
the water not being there at all. The water now resists with the square of
the speed through it, as water does: a violent entry is all but stopped
inside half a second, while the gentle swing and the few metres of carry
that make a drowning a beat rather than a freeze-frame are untouched.
