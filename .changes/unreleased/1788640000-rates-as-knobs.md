type: Changed
title: A cooler game on a phone
---

The game now solves the world sixty times a second instead of a hundred and twenty, the drivers re-read the road thirty, phones draw at most sixty frames a second, and the rear-view mirror refreshes thirty times a second and only looks as far back as a car you could actually be caught by. Nothing about the driving changed — the same pace, the same corners, the same slides — there is simply a great deal less work for a phone to get warm doing. Every one of those rates is now a single named number, so any of them can be moved.
