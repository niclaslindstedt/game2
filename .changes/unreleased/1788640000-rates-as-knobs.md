title: A cooler game on a phone
---

Phones now draw at most sixty frames a second, and the rear-view mirror refreshes thirty times a second instead of on every frame — it also only looks as far back as a car you could actually be caught by, rather than half a stage. The strip over the road shows the same thing; the phone simply stops drawing the whole world a second time to keep it there, which is where most of the heat was coming from. The driving is untouched. Every rate involved is now a single named number, the engine's own two included, so any of them can be moved.
