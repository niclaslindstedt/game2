type: Changed
title: Damage reads off the car, not off bars
---

The damage instrument is one top-view car instead of a car plus five power
bars, and it sits over the rev counter where the eye already is. Each
system is drawn as the part it is — the engine block under the bonnet, the
rack across the front axle, the gearbox down the tunnel, the suspension at
the four wheels — and takes color as it fails, yellow folding to red, while
the shell's own outline carries the chassis. A sound part reads as quiet
steel, so a glance finds the one part that is wrong instead of scanning a
row of full green bars.
