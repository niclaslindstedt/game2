title: A broken car now drives like one
---

Everything the damage instrument shows reaches the wheels. A spent chassis
loses grip and brakes, drags, and — folded harder down one side — pulls
that way with the wheel dead straight, so you hold a correction down every
straight. An engine past saving misfires instead of just making less power,
a gearbox past saving stops taking its top gear, the wing you left in a
ditch is downforce the back of the car no longer has, and every panel torn
off costs pace. Nothing parks the car: a wreck still gets home, at about a
third of the pace it started with.
