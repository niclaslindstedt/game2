---
type: Added
title: Checkpoints
---

Every stage is now split by checkpoints — a board roughly every fifteen
seconds of driving, always just past the exit of a corner and preferring the
tight ones. Driving through one puts your split on screen under the race
clock, measured against your ghost's time at the same board, and marks it on
the minimap. Drowning, or asking for the road back, now returns the car to
the last checkpoint instead of to where it left the road; the wedge rescue
still puts a pinned car back where it stands.
