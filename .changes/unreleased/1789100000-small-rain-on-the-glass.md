title: The rain on the windscreen is life-sized, and it runs in rivulets
---

From the driver's seat the wet glass is now carpeted with beads a millimetre or two across — each a dark-rimmed lens with a point of sky in it — rather than a few bubbles the size of coins. The drops that grow big enough to run leave a thin continuous thread of water behind them down a lane cleared through the beading, the way water actually goes over a screen, and the beading swells with the weather: a downpour's drops are bigger than a drizzle's. The door windows carry the same rain at the same real size, which from the seat beside them is larger on screen. The pass that draws it is now part of the EFFECTS budget: on LOW detail, or with EFFECTS turned down, the glass wets but the rain is not drawn.
