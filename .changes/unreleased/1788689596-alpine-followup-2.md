title: Rounded fill crests the ground can draw, and no two branches stacked down one face
---

An embankment's side now leaves the road's lip through a rounded crest
instead of a kink, so the ground tiles beside a road on a fill no longer
sag a metre or more under its edge every cell — the step in the verge and
the trench under the ribbon's edge on every country. And a corner is no
longer given a junction where its abandoned branch would run down a
hillside stacked over another branch closer than the ground between them
can climb.
