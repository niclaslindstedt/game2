title: A rolling car no longer sinks into the ground, and keeps rolling the way the world says
---

A car going over is drawn at a pitch as well as a roll, but the shape the
roll stood it on was a section ACROSS the car — a width and a height, no
length at all. So the nose and the tail had nothing holding them up: at the
pitch a rollover routinely reaches, more than a metre of car was below the
ground it was supposed to be lying on, and out in the wild a body rolling
into rising ground buried whichever end reached the rise first. The body is
now stood on its whole box, over the ground under all of it rather than the
one point under its middle.

The roll also went on turning the same way in the CAR's frame however far
the car had swung round underneath it — so a body that swapped ends
mid-accident carried on rolling backwards relative to where it was
travelling, which nothing falling can do. The roll it is carrying is now
carried through the yaw, so it keeps going over the same way in the world.
