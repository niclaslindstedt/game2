title: The rear-driver's rear glass is the three-door's, not a box
---

The rear-wheel-drive car's rear side window is a right trapezoid — its top edge runs level from the door pillar to the roof's end, then one long diagonal drops to the sill — and the roof itself ends over the rear seats, so the backlight lies down behind it as one long shallow pane to the tailgate's edge with a slim diagonal pillar beside it, and the wing's posts stand on that glass.
