title: The rear-driver's rear glass is the three-door's, not a box
---

The rear-wheel-drive car's rear side window now has the real car's shape — its top edge runs level from the door pillar to the roof's end, then a long diagonal that turns down into a rounded rear corner — the roof ends over the rear seats so the backlight lies down behind it as one long shallow pane between painted pillars to the tailgate's edge under a deeper blade, the bumpers wrap back to the arches as one moulding instead of a stair of plates, and the door handle is on the door. It is painted like a race car: a white roof instead of the red one, and twin red stripes down the bonnet and over the roof.
