title: The rear-driver's rear glass is the three-door's, not a box
---

The rear-wheel-drive car's rear side window is a right trapezoid — its top edge runs level from the door pillar for a short way and then one long diagonal drops to the sill, leaving the blank sail panel behind it — and its roof ends early so the backlight lies down as one long shallow pane to the tailgate's edge, where the wing's posts now stand on the glass.
