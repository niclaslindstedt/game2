type: Fixed
title: The camera no longer bumps over the road's wheel tracks
---

The road is built with a crown down the middle and two worn wheel tracks
either side of it, and the car rides that cross-section — so a corner that
carried the car across the road heaved the whole picture up and down on
stages that were dead flat. The cameras now hang off the car on a little
play: a wheel track, the crown and the step down onto the verge are absorbed,
while crests, landings, cliffs and jumps still move the shot at full size.
From the driver's seat the horizon stays level through a wheel track too, and
still leans with a banked corner.
