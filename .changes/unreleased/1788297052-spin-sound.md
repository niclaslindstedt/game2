title: A spin you can hear
---

Losing a drift now makes a noise. Past the angle where the front tyres have
nothing left to pull against, all four are dragged sideways at once — and
that arrives as a swell of scrub with the mass of the car under it, sized by
how fast the car was going when it let go. It is deliberately not an impact:
nothing broke, and a spin is a mistake to learn from rather than damage to
be afraid of.
