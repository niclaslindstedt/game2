title: Every gearbox is a real one now
---

Each car's gears are worked out the way a drivetrain makes them: a gear's ceiling is the shift rpm through that ratio and the final drive on that tyre, and a gear's pull is the same ratio multiplying the engine's torque over the wheel and the car's weight. So the ladders have different lengths and different gear counts — the saloon has four gears where the other two have five — and the shove falls away steeply through them instead of holding flat to the top. The racing set is a trade again too: six per cent taller gears cost six per cent of the shove, and the five per cent it hands back is read against that rather than on top of it.
