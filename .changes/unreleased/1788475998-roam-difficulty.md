type: Added
title: A difficulty slider on Roam
---

Roam's stage settings now open with a DIFFICULTY slider, from GENTLE to SAVAGE. Turning it up builds a harder road out of the same seed: more corners and tighter ones, jumps that throw the car further, bigger country either side to fall down, and a narrower road to keep it on. The ROAD width row is gone — the slider owns it.
