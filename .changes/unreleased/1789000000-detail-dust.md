title: DETAIL now decides whose wheels raise dust
---

The DETAIL row in OPTIONS ▸ PICTURE has gained a fifth lever, and it answers
a question about WHO rather than about how much: which cars on the road hang
dust over the stage — the cloud a car tows, the grit and clods its wheels
kick up, and the stones a slide throws out sideways.

LOW takes all of it off every car, which is the setting a phone that
stutters has been asking for: three particle pools with nothing spawning
into them and their draw calls gone with them. MEDIUM leaves it to the car
you are driving, because that cloud is what a loose surface feels like from
the seat, and puts the field's shared plume away — a rival's dust is worth a
great deal to the picture and nothing at all to the driving, so it is the
half a struggling machine gives up first. HIGH is the whole entry list
towing dust the way a rally actually looks.

Unlike the rest of the row it applies the instant it is set rather than at
the next stage, and it reaches nothing but the ground: a crash still ploughs
up the country, a landing still punches a puff out from under all four
wheels, and a locked tyre on tarmac still smokes.
