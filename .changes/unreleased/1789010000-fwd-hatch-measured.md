title: The front-driver measured again — a real door, a plumb pillar, period wheels
---

The front-wheel-drive hatch is put back against a ruled side elevation: a 1.2 m door whose rear edge is one straight line under a B-pillar that stands plumb, a quarter glass nearly as long behind it under the wide C-pillar this shape is known for, a roof that runs past the rear axle over a steeper tailgate, glass that runs closer to the door tops, fifteen-inch gravel wheels on tall sidewalls with lattice rims in place of the modern low-profile ones, tail lamps up under the tailgate's foot, and both bumpers a hand higher with the skirts running down under them.
