title: The four-wheel-drive rebuilt as a measured four-door works sedan
---

The all-wheel-drive car is a four-door turbo sedan in the Group A idiom now, measured off a ruled period side elevation rather than judged: a long wedge of a bonnet off a low nose, a forward greenhouse on thin posts with a plumb B-pillar and two doors a side, a raked backlight onto a short high boot with a lip on it, pressed blisters over fifteen-inch gravel wheels on broad white five-spoke rims, two scoops on the roof, four lamp pods on the nose, and red mud flaps. The back is spent on: one full-width band of lamps with the plate let into it, a deep body-colour bumper over a black valance, and the roof's black carried down the rear posts, across the quarters and off the tail, with a red diagonal across the rear door where the white ends.
