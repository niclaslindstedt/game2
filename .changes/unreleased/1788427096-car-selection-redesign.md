type: Changed
title: The car selection screen is mostly car now
---

The pre-race card has been rebuilt around the thing it exists to choose. The
car turns on a stand that takes the column that grows with the screen, framed
from its own measured size so a phone's tall pane and a laptop's wide one both
come back full of car; its name stands across the head of the frame, its drive
layout beside it, and its one line of billing across the foot.

The spec sheet went from eight axes to four. Mass, gears, braking and turn-in
are gone — nobody chooses a car on them — and traction, tarmac grip and gravel
grip are one GRIP bar. DRIFTING is new, and it reads off the drivetrain, so the
rear-driver tops it the way it does on the road. Acceleration and top speed keep
both a bar and a figure: one says what the car is, the other what it is against
the roster.

TRANSMISSION now leads the column beside the car as two boxes rather than a pair
of chips under a footnote, and flipping it is something you watch: the top speed
and the 0–100 count to their new values while the bars slide under them.
