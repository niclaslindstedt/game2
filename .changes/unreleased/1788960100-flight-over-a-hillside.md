type: Fixed
title: Cars no longer fly through a hillside
---

A car knocked into a flight on a slope — clipping a rock off the road, say —
tilted its nose down into the hill whichever way it was actually travelling,
and then went on flying with an end of itself buried in the ground until its
MIDDLE reached the surface. It now lies along its arc the right way round
(the nose comes up when the tail is leading) and it lands the moment the body
meets the hill, not the moment its middle does.
