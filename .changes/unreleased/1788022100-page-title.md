type: Changed
title: The browser tab just says Scandinavian Flick
---

The page title was the name with a tagline bolted on after a dash, and a
browser tab shows about thirty characters before it gives up — so what
anybody actually read was the name, a dash, and the first word or two of a
sentence that went nowhere. It is now just the name. The share card a link
unfurls into has room for the longer line and keeps it.

The installed app is now captioned **ScanFlick** rather than Flick. A
launcher gives a caption about twelve characters and this is nine, so both
halves of the name survive where before only the second one did — an icon
captioned Flick could have been anything.
