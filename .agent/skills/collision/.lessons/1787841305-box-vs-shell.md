`TUNING.collision.halfLength/halfWidth` is one box for the whole catalog
and has to contain every drawn shell. The number that matters is NOT the
profile's first/last station: bumpers stand proud of both caps (roughly
`depth − 0.02` past them), and the bumper is what meets the tree. An air
dam authored with its own depth will also reach past the nose unless it is
placed to tuck UNDER the bumper, which is what `buildFront` now does.

`tests/car_geometry_test.ts` checks both ends of this and is the only test
in the repo that imports from `pwa/` — it can, because `car-styles.ts` and
the `bodyHalfLength`/`bodyHalfWidth` probes in `car/shell.ts` are pure data
and pure arithmetic with no three.js and no DOM. It also asserts the box is
not wastefully LARGER than the biggest car, because a box well past every
shell scrapes on thin air.

The catalog currently sits exactly on `halfLength` 2.1, so a new car has
essentially no room in length; widen the tuning number (and re-run
`make sim`) rather than shaving a spec to fit.
