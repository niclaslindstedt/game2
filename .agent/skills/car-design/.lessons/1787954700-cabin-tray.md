Two surfaces bound everything an interior can show, and neither is the one
you would reach for. `buildShell` lofts a CLOSED body, so there is an opaque
top deck running the length of the car at about the belt line: everything
below it is inside a sealed shell and cannot be seen from anywhere. And the
side glass starts at the window sill a few centimetres above that. What is
left is a tray roughly 350 mm deep — the height of the glass, and nothing
else.

So a cabin stacked up from `spec.floorY` at honest human proportions comes
out entirely below the glass: a seat cushion at 0.52 puts the helmets at
0.99–1.25 against a window band starting at 1.03, and the whole crew is
behind the door skin. Anchor to `sillY` instead and hang everything under
it (shoulders AT the sill, helmet centre ~140 mm over it, seat back and
bulkhead down to the pan), and it lands first time on all three bodies.

Two corollaries. Lay a dark PAN over the body's deck, or the cabin floor is
sunlit body paint and the brightest thing in the car. And nothing below that
pan is worth triangles — the door bars a real cage carries run under the
sill, where no camera outside the car will ever see them.

The way to find any of this is a raycast through the glass from where the
camera actually stands, printing what it hits in order. Bounding boxes lie
here: a union over the lining strips spans the full panel whether or not the
windows are cut out of it.
