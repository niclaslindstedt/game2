---
title: Hand-wound round geometry hides culled-face bugs — use THREE primitives + bakeShading for anything circular
date: 2026-08-26
scope: pwa/src/game/car-body.ts
concepts: [winding, wheels, baked-shading]
---

The first wheel was hand-authored rings (tread + sidewall + hub fan) and
shipped two invisible-face bugs that only showed as "broken fan" artifacts
in the contact sheet. The loft's ring convention (CCW seen from the nose,
quads as a[k+1], a[k], b[k], b[k+1]) is easy to get right ONCE for the
shell, but re-deriving winding for each round part fails silently — faces
are culled, not flagged. `bakeShading(geometry, color)` exists so round
parts can come from THREE primitives (CylinderGeometry etc., rotated onto
the right axis) with correct winding by construction and still match the
baked-sun look. Reach for it before hand-authoring any circular part.
