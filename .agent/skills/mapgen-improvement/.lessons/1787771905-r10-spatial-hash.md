---
title: The R10 probe must be spatially hashed — a linear scan makes long bands take seconds per stage
date: 2026-08-26
scope: engine/mapgen/generate.ts
concepts: [search, performance, self-distance, spatial-hash]
---

`clearsSelf` as candidate-points × all-committed-points is O(n²) over the
stage: fine at 2 km, but 5–12 km bands spent 0.4–3.4 s per stage (multiplied
by sub-seed rejects). The fix that shipped: `createPointField` — probe points
bucketed in a grid with cell size = `minSelfDistance`, so each candidate
checks 3×3 cells. Two companions matter at long lengths: backtrack THREE
segments when boxed in (a one-segment retreat re-enters the same pocket),
and an R5 same-direction ANGLE cap (`maxSameDirectionAngle`) that kills
near-loop candidates before the geometry is built and probed — most doomed
draws were two tight same-dir turns summing past ~200°. After all three,
xlong (10.4–11.8 km) generates in ~80 ms average, worst ~540 ms, across a
120-seed sweep.
